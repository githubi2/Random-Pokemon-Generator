Card level

Pokemon Card Release Dates: When Cards Actually Hit Shelves

A set's release date is really a week, not a day. Most expansions open with pre-release events at local game stores about two weeks before the official date — Chaos Rising ran its pre-releases May 9-17 before arriving on May 22 — and then the main wave lands everywhere at once: booster boxes, Elite Trainer Boxes and booster bundles all ship on the same day.

Special sets do it differently. Ascended Heroes and 30th Celebration arrived through collection products rather than a standard booster box, and their supporting products can spread over the following weeks instead of landing on one day. Promo cards run on their own schedules too — store promos are handed out at play events, not sold on release day. So when you are planning a purchase, the date in the table above tells you when the main wave hits; the notes flag anything unusual about a specific set.

How it works

How Pokemon TCG Expansions and Set Releases Work

Three things explain almost every date on this page: the era's eight-week heart, the Japan-to-English lag, and the small print that lets dates move.

The eight-week rhythm (roughly)

Between September 2025 and November 2026, the seven gaps between headline sets run 49, 77, 56, 56, 56, 61 and 51 days — the 56-day (eight-week) gap shows up three times, and no gap has stretched past eleven weeks. A new set every seven to eleven weeks is what the era has actually delivered, with eight weeks as the default. That is why a "when is the next set" answer is usually possible even months ahead: the cadence itself is the tell.

Japan runs ahead, with one big exception

Japanese releases have historically led English ones by about two to three months: Storm Emeralda landed on July 31, 2026, and its English counterpart, Delta Reign, follows on November 6. The two versions share cards but not always names, which is why a set can be fully known months before it arrives. September 2026 broke the pattern for the first time — 30th Celebration launched worldwide on a single day.

Announcements lead, and dates can move

New sets are usually announced two to three months before release, with Build & Battle pre-release weekends in the final stretch. If something goes wrong, a date can shift: the EMEA rollout of Mega Evolution slid from September 26 to October 10, 2025. That is why every date here carries the same standard — officially announced by The Pokemon Company, or it does not make the table.

FAQ

Pokemon Release Dates FAQ

When is the next Pokemon TCG set?

Mega Evolution—Delta Reign releases on November 6, 2026. It is the next main expansion after 30th Celebration (September 16, 2026) and headlines Mega Rayquaza ex.

What is the newest Pokemon TCG set?

30th Celebration, released September 16, 2026 — an all-foil anniversary set that was the first Pokemon TCG product to launch worldwide on a single day.

How often do new Pokemon TCG sets come out?

Roughly every seven to eleven weeks, with eight weeks as the default — three of the era's seven gaps so far were exactly 56 days. That pace works out to six or seven sets a year once special sets like Ascended Heroes are counted.

Do Pokemon TCG release dates ever change?

Yes, though it is rare. Mega Evolution's EMEA rollout was pushed to October 10, 2025 by a delivery delay, and dates for unreleased sets can still move. Every date on this page is one The Pokemon Company has announced — nothing speculative — and the list is re-checked every month.

How far ahead of English releases do Japanese sets come out?

Historically about two to three months. Storm Emeralda came out in Japan on July 31, 2026, with its English counterpart (Delta Reign) following on November 6. 30th Celebration in September 2026 was the first time both launches happened on the same day.

What are all the Pokemon TCG sets in order?

The Mega Evolution era so far, oldest first: Mega Evolution, Phantasmal Flames, Ascended Heroes, Perfect Order, Chaos Rising, Pitch Black, 30th Celebration — with Delta Reign next on November 6, 2026.

What is a special set?

A set that sits outside the numbered main expansions, usually with a different product build. Ascended Heroes (295 cards) and 30th Celebration are the two special sets of this era; everything else in the table is a main expansion.

What Pokemon set is after Delta Reign?

Nothing is announced yet — 2027's calendar is still empty, and this page only lists officially announced sets. When the next one is confirmed it gets added here with its date.

What Pokemon sets are released in 2026?

2026's slate: four main expansions — Perfect Order (March 27), Chaos Rising (May 22), Pitch Black (July 17) and Delta Reign (November 6) — plus two special sets, Ascended Heroes (January 30) and 30th Celebration (September 16).

How often is this page updated?

The calendar is checked against official announcements at least once a month, and every confirmed change is recorded in the update history at the bottom of this page.
