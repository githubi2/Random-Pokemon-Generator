Privacy Policy - Random Pokemon Generator

Last updated: September 5, 2026. This policy explains exactly what Random Pokemon Generator does — and does not do — with your information, in plain language. The short version: we collect almost nothing, and everything you generate stays on your own device.

TL;DR

The Short Version

We collect no personal information

Random Pokemon Generator has no accounts, no registration, no newsletters, and no contact forms. We never ask for your name, email address, age, location, or any other personal detail. You can use every tool on the site — the main Random Pokemon Generator, the Nuzlocke Team Builder, and every other page — completely anonymously.

Your rolls live in your browser

Roll history, saved teams, and filter preferences are stored in your browser's local storage on your own device. That data is never uploaded to us, never synced anywhere, and disappears the moment you clear your browser's site data.

One analytics tool, easy to block

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand aggregate traffic — which pages are popular and roughly how many visitors we get. It uses cookies, and you can block it entirely with a browser setting or add-on without breaking anything on the site.

Data minimization

Information We Do Not Collect

No account data

Because there is no sign-up system, there is no account database. We do not store usernames, passwords, email addresses, or profile information of any kind — there is simply nothing to leak, sell, or breach.

No gameplay data on our servers

Everything you generate is computed locally by JavaScript running in your browser. When you spin the Pokemon Wheel Spinner, build a squad in the Pokemon Team Picker, or judge the dex in Pokemon Smash or Pass, the results are produced on your device and stay there. We have no server-side database of user activity.

No payments, no tracking pixels

The site has no checkout, no advertising network, no affiliate tracking pixels, and no social media trackers. There is nothing to monetize your behavior with, and no third-party ad company ever sees your visit.

On your device only

Local Browser Storage

What is stored, and why

Several tools remember your recent activity so you can pick up where you left off. The Random Pokemon Generator keeps your last 20 rolls in its Roll History; the Nuzlocke Team Builder stores rolled squads with replay links; the Random Shiny Pokemon Generator and Random Mega Pokemon Generator keep their own roll histories; the Pokemon Team Picker remembers your squad; the Random Pokemon Name Generator keeps nickname preferences; and Who's That Pokemon stores game progress and scores.

Where it goes (nowhere)

All of this uses your browser's localStorage, a storage area that lives entirely on your device. The data is never transmitted to our servers, never backed up anywhere, and never accessible to us. Share links encode your squad in the URL itself — opening one just lets the recipient's browser reproduce the same roll locally. Reference pages like Pokemon Shiny Odds and the Pokemon Nature Chart store nothing at all beyond the same pattern of optional local preferences.

How to delete it

Clear your browser's site data or local storage for this domain and everything is gone permanently. In most browsers: Settings → Privacy → Site settings → View permissions and data stored across sites, then delete this site's entry. Most tools also include their own Clear History button inside the history panel.

Anonymous statistics

Analytics (Google Analytics 4)

What it collects

We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to measure aggregate, anonymous usage: page views, approximate geographic region (country/city level), device and browser type, and how visitors move between pages. GA4 sets first-party cookies (such as _ga) to distinguish returning visitors from new ones. We do not use it to identify you personally, and we do not combine it with any other dataset.

Who processes it

Google acts as the data processor for this analytics data, under its own privacy terms. You can read Google's policy at policies.google.com/privacy. We do not enable advertising features, remarketing, or personalized ads in our GA4 configuration.

How to opt out

You can block analytics in any of three ways: disable or block cookies for this site in your browser settings; install Google's official Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on; or use any content blocker that filters Google Analytics. Every tool on this site works identically with analytics fully blocked.

Infrastructure

Third-Party Services & Content

PokeAPI (Pokemon data and artwork)

Pokemon species data and official artwork are loaded from PokeAPI and its image CDN. When your browser requests a sprite or dataset, those servers see a standard resource request (your IP address and browser user agent), the same as loading any image on the web. We send them no personal information about you.

Vercel (hosting)

The site is hosted on Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel's edge network processes standard server logs — IP address, requested URL, timestamp, and user agent — to deliver pages and protect against abuse. These logs are retained according to Vercel's own policies.

Directory badges and outbound links

The homepage footer displays small "Featured On" badges loaded from third-party directory sites (such as DANG!, SaaS Roots, and similar directories). Loading those badge images tells those servers that a page on our site was viewed. Outbound links to external sites are governed by those sites' own privacy policies, which we encourage you to read.

You are in control

Your Choices & Controls

Delete your local data anytime

Because your roll data lives only in your browser, you hold the only copy. Clear the site's local storage and it is permanently erased — there is no server-side copy for you to request deletion of, and no account to close.

Block analytics and cookies

Block this site's cookies, install Google's opt-out add-on, or run a content blocker. Nothing on the site requires cookies to function; every generator, game, and calculator works with storage and analytics fully disabled (history features simply will not persist between visits if storage is blocked).

Do Not Track

The site itself performs no cross-site tracking, so there is nothing for us to stop. Google Analytics does not respond to the Do Not Track signal by default; use the opt-out methods above if you prefer not to be counted.

The fine print

Children, Changes & Contact

Children's privacy

The site is a general-audience fan tool and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Since there are no accounts or forms, no personal information is gathered from any visitor regardless of age.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the site handles data — for example by adding a new analytics or hosting provider — we will update this page and revise the "Last updated" date at the top. Significant changes will be summarized in the page's commit history on our public repository.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Open an issue on our public GitHub repository at github.com/githubi2/Random-Pokemon-Generator — it is the fastest way to reach the maintainers. Random Pokemon Generator is a fan project and is not affiliated with Nintendo, Game Freak, or The Pokemon Company.

FAQ

Privacy Policy FAQ

Does Random Pokemon Generator collect my personal information?

No. The site has no accounts, no sign-up forms, no comment systems, and no contact forms, so it never asks for your name, email address, or any other personal detail. Every tool runs entirely in your browser.

Where is my roll history and team data stored?

In your own browser's local storage, on your own device. Roll history, saved teams, and filter preferences never leave your device and are never transmitted to our servers. Clearing your browser's site data deletes them permanently.

What cookies does this site use?

The site itself sets no cookies. Google Analytics 4, our anonymous usage statistics tool, sets first-party cookies such as _ga to distinguish visitors. You can block these cookies in your browser settings or with Google's opt-out add-on without breaking any tool.

How do I opt out of analytics tracking?

Three ways: block cookies for this site in your browser settings, install the official Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on, or use a content blocker that filters Google Analytics. All tools on the site work identically with analytics blocked.

Does this site share data with third parties?

We do not sell, rent, or share any user data. Two third parties process limited technical data as part of serving the site: Google (anonymous analytics) and Vercel (hosting logs). Pokemon data and artwork are loaded from the public PokeAPI project, which sees only a standard resource request.
