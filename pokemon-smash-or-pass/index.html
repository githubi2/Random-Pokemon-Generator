    How it works

    How to Play Pokemon Smash or Pass

    The smash or pass game, explained

    Smash or pass is a viral judgment game that spread through streams and short videos: a Pokémon appears, and you decide in a second whether you would take it (smash) or skip it (pass). The fun is in the fast verdicts, the weird Pokémon you land on, and watching your own taste show up in the stats. This generator keeps the classic loop — one Pokémon, two buttons, next — and adds streak counting and a shareable verdict score.

    Play it in three taps

    Hit Start to roll the first Pokémon. Read its name, type tags, and Gen badge, then tap 💖 Smash or 🚫 Pass — or press S and P on your keyboard to play faster. The next Pokémon appears instantly, and the counter keeps your smash rate, your current streak, and a rolling history of every verdict. No account, no install, no limit: keep judging for as long as you want.

    Take the all-Pokémon challenge

    For the full experience, run an all-Pokémon smash-or-pass challenge: judge the complete list of 1,025 species plus regional forms from Gen 1 to Gen 9. Rerolling is automatic, and the game never repeats a Pokémon until you have seen the whole pool. Compare your final smash rate with friends, or limit the challenge to one region for a quicker run.

    Build your smash list

    Every smash is a vote for your favorite Pokémon — watch your list grow as you play. The Verdict History panel shows every judgment with its artwork, so you can review your taste at the end of a session. Fans use the list to argue with friends, prep tier-list videos, or settle the eternal how-picky-are-you debate once and for all.

    Made for streams and shorts

    Smash or pass is built for cameras. The keyboard shortcuts (S / P) let streamers keep the verdicts fast, the instant streak counter creates natural drama, and the rare shiny rolls (about 1 in 12) make for perfect clip moments. Play it live with chat voting, or record a session and post the reaction.

    The card also works as a pocket pokemon type coverage calculator: the type coverage row under the data table shows exactly what the rolled Pokémon is weak to, what it resists, and what it is immune to — computed on the fly from the full 18-type chart. Before you smash or pass, you can spot a 4x weakness or a clean immunity in one glance, which makes the game a handy matchup trainer on top of the fun.

    House rules and style

    Part of the fun is inventing your own rules. Ban the starters, judge only unevolved Pokémon, require a 5-second timer per verdict, or make the whole chat vote before you tap. Nuzlocke players run it as a brutal filter: whichever Pokémon you smash must join your team — pair it with the Nuzlocke Team Builder for the full ruleset. The verdict buttons and keyboard shortcuts keep the pace fast enough for any house rule.

    Play it anywhere

    Smash or pass runs in any browser with no sign-up, no install, and no loading screens — judge Pokémon on your phone during a break or on a big screen for a party. The random order and streak counter reset any time you tap Start again, so every session feels fresh. It is the fastest way to burn ten minutes arguing with friends about how picky you really are. Prefer building over judging? Use the random Pokemon generator for full teams, or spin the Pokemon Wheel Spinner and let fate pick for you. When the verdicts go quiet, name the mystery Pokémon hiding behind a silhouette in the Who's That Pokemon guessing game. And when a favourite finally earns a spot on your squad, give it a proper name with the Random Pokemon Name Generator. And when you would rather roll pure power, the Random Mega Pokemon Generator deals nothing but Mega Evolutions. And if you would rather judge rare recolors, the Random Shiny Pokemon Generator rolls nothing but shiny Pokemon. Wondering how rare that actually is? Pokemon Shiny Odds has every rate and a calculator.

    Share your verdict score

    When your session ends, hit Share My Verdicts. The generator copies a summary like 'I smashed 23/30 Pokémon on Random Pokemon Generator — how picky are you?' with a link your friends can open and play themselves. Post it in Discord, Reddit, or as a comment on a TikTok — the game travels with the score.

    House rules keep it fresh

    The verdict loop stays fun long after the first session if you add your own rules. Try a no-legendaries draft, a generation-locked region run, or the café rule where you only smash Pokémon that would fit your dream team. The Game Settings panel was built for exactly this: switch between Random Order and Pokédex Order to quiz your memory in the same sequence as the games, tick the types or regions you want to judge, and flip the legendary and mythical switch off for a grounded run. Whatever the house rule, the streak counter and the shareable verdict score turn every session into something worth posting — and every post into a new session worth playing. The verdict buttons echo that flexibility too: click, swipe, or press S and P — three ways to answer the same question fast, whether you are judging alone or with a room full of trainers. Settle the debate in the comments: the link your friend opens runs the same game, the same pool, and the same rules. Once the debate is settled, take your favourites into the Pokemon Team Picker and turn them into a real six-Pokemon team with natures, moves and items.

    FAQ

    Pokemon Smash or Pass FAQ

    What is Pokemon smash or pass?

    Smash or pass is a viral game where a random Pokemon appears and you decide: smash (you would take it) or pass (you would not). This generator turns it into a one-click game across all 1,025 Pokemon from Gen 1-9.

    How do I play the smash or pass game?

    Hit Start, read the Pokemon that appears, then tap Smash or Pass (or press S and P on your keyboard). The game rolls the next Pokemon instantly and counts your verdicts, streak, and smash rate.

    Can I filter by generation?

    Yes. Tap any generation chip (Gen 1 through Gen 9) before starting to judge only Pokemon from that region — Kanto only, Paldea only, or the full roster.

    Is there a list of all Pokemon?

    Yes — the game draws from the complete list of 1,025 Pokemon plus regional forms across Gen 1-9, with official artwork and type tags on every card.

    Can I share my results?

    One tap. The Share button copies a verdict summary like 'I smashed 23/30 Pokemon on Random Pokemon Generator — how picky are you?' with the link, ready for Reddit, Discord, or social media.

    Do shinies appear?

    Sometimes. About 1 in 12 rolls can be a shiny Pokemon with sparkling artwork — smash it or pass it, your call.
