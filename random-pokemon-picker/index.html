    How it works

    How the Random Pokemon Picker Works

    One click, one Pokemon

    The Random Pokemon Picker is the fastest way to draw a Pokemon: choose how many you want (one to twelve), narrow the pool if you like, and press Pick Random Pokemon. Every result comes straight from the full national dex — 1,025 species across Generation 1 through 9, with no repeats inside a single pick. The card shows the official artwork, type tags, national dex number and base stat total at a glance, so you can judge the draw in a second.

    Each pick also comes with a sound: the Pokemon's own cry plays the moment the card appears, and clicking any card plays it again. That instant audio makes the draw feel like a real reveal — perfect when you are rolling a starter with friends or deciding a league assignment on a call. Where the Random Pokemon Generator packs in deep filters and a tactical report for every squad, this page exists for speed: pick, hear, move on.

    Narrow the pool without the clutter

    Three filter dimensions keep the picker light: Generation (Gen 1 through Gen 9, any combination), Type (any of 18 types), and rarity (Legendary and Mythical each set to Include, Exclude or Only). Want a Kanto starter? Lock Gen 1. Facing a dark-type gym and need a counter? Pick Fairy or Fighting under Type (any of). Running a casual league that bans box legendaries? Flip Legendary and Mythical to Exclude — the pool counter drops live, so you always know exactly what the next draw can roll.

    The same filters power bigger jobs. Draw six from Gen 1 only for a Kanto run, or filter to a single type and a single generation for a themed squad. For a stricter ruleset, roll a starter here then continue the challenge with the Nuzlocke Team Builder. If you want an animated, watchable reveal instead of a silent roll, the Pokemon Wheel Spinner spins on screen — and if you want mega-power on purpose, the Random Mega Pokemon Generator rolls only Mega Evolutions, while the Random Shiny Pokemon Generator draws only shiny-coloration Pokemon.

    Share the exact pick

    Every pick is reproducible. The Copy Pick Link button builds a URL that encodes both your current filters and the exact Pokemon drawn — shiny variants included. Send it to a friend, a stream chat or a Discord channel and everyone opens the identical picks, then rolls again under the same rules. It is the fastest way to turn a random draw into a shared moment.

    Once a pick lands, the rest of the suite takes over. Name the member with the Random Pokemon Name Generator, check its best nature with the Pokemon Nature Chart, or put it on paper with the Pokemon Card Generator — complete with a Pokemon Trainer Card Generator card for the trainer. For a full hand-built squad around that pick, the Pokemon Team Picker gives you every slot under manual control.

    Play around the pick

    The picker also feeds the games. Test your memory with the Who's That Pokemon guessing game, or judge every result one verdict at a time with Pokemon Smash or Pass. If you are hunting sparkles for real, Pokemon Shiny Odds computes your actual chances per method — and the Blog walks the math behind the odds, natures, and challenge runs in full.

    Everything runs in your browser: no accounts, no signup, nothing uploaded. Your picks stay on screen until you roll again, and Reset All returns every filter to defaults in one tap.

    FAQ

    Random Pokemon Picker FAQ

    What is a random Pokemon picker?

    A random Pokemon picker is a tool that draws one or more Pokemon at random from the full national dex so you can settle a starter, fill a team slot, or start a challenge. This one picks from all 1,025 species across Generation 1 through 9, with optional filters for generation, type, Legendary and Mythical status.

    How many Pokemon can the picker draw at once?

    From one to twelve per pick, controlled by the Count slider. One is for a single decision — a starter, a first Nuzlocke encounter, a league assignment. Six or more covers a whole squad. No repeats inside a single pick.

    What filters can I use with the picker?

    Three dimensions, kept light on purpose: Generation (Gen 1 through 9, any combination), Type (any of 18 types), and rarity (Legendary and Mythical each set to Include, Exclude or Only). The pool counter updates live so you always know how many Pokemon the next pick can draw from.

    Can the picker generate shiny Pokemon?

    Yes. Shiny mode has three options: Normal, 8% Random, or Shiny (always shiny). In 8% Random mode each pick has an independent 8% chance to come up shiny — a style-flavored roll, not a real hunt. For real catch odds, see the Pokemon Shiny Odds page.

    How do I share the Pokemon I picked?

    Press Copy Pick Link. It builds a URL that encodes both your current filters and the exact Pokemon drawn — shiny variants included. Anyone opening that link sees the identical picks, and can press Pick again under the same rules.

    Which Pokemon are included in the picker?

    Every species from Generation 1 through Generation 9 — 1,025 Pokemon plus regional and special forms — with official artwork, base stats, types and abilities sourced from PokeAPI. Generation and type filters apply to the base species list.
