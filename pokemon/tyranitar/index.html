#248 · Rock / Dark · Armor Pokemon · Gen 2

Tyranitar

Tyranitar is a 202 kg wall of armor that walks into a battle and changes the weather on the way in. Sand Stream is not decoration: under sand its 100 Special Defense counts as 150, which is the difference between a Pokemon that takes a hit and one that shrugs it off. The other half of the story is a 4x Fighting weakness that every opponent knows about.

The Armor Pokemon

Tyranitar Pokemon: Sand, Bulk and the Fighting Problem

What Tyranitar is, in one paragraph

Number 248 is a Rock and Dark type from Johto, 2 metres tall and 202 kilograms, and it has been the anchor of sand teams since the weather mechanic existed. The whole Larvitar line totals 600, which is the club that pseudo-legendaries belong to, and Tyranitar is the slowest member of that club by a wide margin.

It looks like a defensive Pokemon and it hits like an offensive one. Attack 134 is the second highest number on the card, and most of the moves it actually runs are physical: Stone Edge, Crunch, Earthquake, and whatever coverage the team needs.

The 4x Fighting weakness, and what it costs

Both of Tyranitar's types are weak to Fighting, so a Close Combat or a Drain Punch arrives at four times normal damage. That single number shapes everything about how the Pokemon gets played. It cannot sit in front of a Fighting type and trade; it has to come in on something else, or come in after the Fighting type is gone.

The practical answer in modern games is Terastallization. Turning into a Fairy or a Flying type removes the 4x hole for as long as the Tera lasts, and it is the standard way to keep Tyranitar alive through a match where the opponent clearly packed a Fighting move for it. The rest of the defensive card is not bad at all: Fire, Poison, Flying, Ghost and Dark are all halved, and Psychic is a clean zero.

Sand Stream is a Special Defense item

Sand Stream summons a sandstorm on switch-in. Most people stop there, at the chip damage, and miss the part that matters: since Gen 4, Rock types get a 1.5x multiplier to Special Defense while sand is up. Tyranitar's base 100 Special Defense is therefore working at roughly 150 in practice, which is legendary-tier bulk on a Pokemon that already has 100 HP and 110 Defense.

It also means Tyranitar takes no sand damage itself, and neither do its Rock and Steel teammates, while the opponent's team loses 1/16 of its HP every turn. That is the entire reason sand teams are built around this one Pokemon rather than around any of the faster Rock types.

Larvitar, Pupitar, Tyranitar

The line is three stages and two level requirements. Larvitar evolves into Pupitar at level 30, and Pupitar evolves into Tyranitar at level 55. In the Johto games the only Larvitar worth hunting sits on Mt. Silver, which means a player has to reach the post-game before the line is even available.

Mega Evolution is the fourth form, and it needs the Tyranitarite rather than a level. Mega Tyranitar keeps Rock and Dark, keeps Sand Stream, and jumps to 700 total: Attack 164, Defense 150, Special Defense 120, Speed 71. Slow, armored, and still carrying the same 4x Fighting weakness, which no amount of Mega Evolution fixes.

Why Speed 61 is not the problem it looks like

Base 61 Speed is slow for a 600 total, and slow Pokemon usually lose. Tyranitar is the exception because its job is to take one hit and answer with a bigger one. Against the bulky offense that dominates most formats, 61 Speed is enough to move before walls, and against faster teams it is simply expected to eat a hit.

The trade is real in one direction: anything with 100+ Speed that carries a Fighting or Ground move can remove it before it acts. That is why Tyranitar sets often run enough bulk to survive a hit rather than enough Speed to avoid one.

Where Tyranitar sits among the 600 club

Four Pokemon on this site share the same 600 total and they are built completely differently. Garchomp is the fast one at 102 Speed. Dragonite sits at 80 with Multiscale soaking the first hit. Metagross has the highest Defense of the group at 130 and the highest Attack at 135. Tyranitar has the lowest Speed at 61 and the only weather ability in the set.

Put the four stat lines next to each other and the design intent is obvious. Tyranitar gives up initiative to buy 100 HP, 110 Defense and a Special Defense that doubles in value under its own sand. It is the only member of the group whose bulk gets better for free, which is why the missing Speed has never been treated as a fatal flaw.

What it beats, and what beats it

Offensively, Rock and Dark together hit a long list for at least neutral damage, and the Dark half means Psychic and Ghost types cannot switch in at all. Against Fire, Flying, Bug and Ice, Stone Edge is a straightforward answer. Against opposing walls, Crunch's chance to drop Defense turns a stalemate into progress.

Defensively the losing matchups are easy to name: Fighting types of any kind, Ground types that carry Earthquake, Steel types that resist both STABs, and bulky Water types that can take a Stone Edge and answer with a boosted Surf. Fairy types are a newer problem than the others, since the type did not exist when Tyranitar was designed.

What Mega Evolution adds, point by point

The Mega form puts 100 points on the card and every one of them goes into something defensive or physical: +40 Defense, +30 Attack, +20 Special Defense, +10 Speed. Nothing goes into Special Attack, which stays at 95, and the ability locks to Sand Stream so the sand is guaranteed on switch-in.

What does not change is more interesting. The typing stays Rock and Dark, so Fighting still lands at 4x, and the Speed only moves from 61 to 71, which is still slower than most of the format. Mega Tyranitar is not a fix for the Pokemon's weaknesses; it is the same Pokemon with a bigger hammer and thicker armor.

FAQ

Tyranitar Pokemon FAQ

What type is Tyranitar?

Rock and Dark. That combination gives it six weaknesses including a 4x hit from Fighting, six resistances, and a full immunity to Psychic.

What is Tyranitar weak to?

Fighting does 4x damage. Ground, Steel, Water, Grass, Bug and Fairy each do 2x. Normal, Fire, Poison, Flying, Ghost and Dark are halved, and Psychic is cancelled completely.

How does Tyranitar evolve?

Larvitar becomes Pupitar at level 30, and Pupitar becomes Tyranitar at level 55. Mega Evolution is separate and needs the Tyranitarite rather than a level.

What abilities does Tyranitar have?

Sand Stream normally, with Unnerve as the hidden ability. Mega Tyranitar always has Sand Stream, so the Mega never loses the sand.

What is Tyranitar's base stat total?

600 in its base form and 700 as a Mega. The Mega version moves those 100 extra points into Attack, Defense, Special Defense and Speed.

Is Tyranitar a legendary Pokemon?

No. It is the final stage of a three-stage evolution line, which makes it a pseudo-legendary: same 600 total as the legendaries, ordinary capture method.

What are Mega Tyranitar's stats?

Attack jumps to 164, Defense to 150 and Special Defense to 120, on top of the same 100 HP. Special Attack stays at 95 and Speed edges up to 71. That is 700 total, and Sand Stream is the only ability it can have.

Where do you catch Larvitar?

In Gold, Silver and their remakes the only reliable spot is Mt. Silver, which is post-game content. Later titles move the line around, so the location depends on which game you are in.

Does sandstorm make Tyranitar stronger?

It does, and not through chip damage. Sand gives every Rock type a 1.5x Special Defense multiplier, so Tyranitar's 100 becomes an effective 150 while the weather holds.

What moves do most Tyranitar sets run?

Stealth Rock, Crunch, Stone Edge and Earthquake is the classic four. The first slot sets hazards, the next two are the STABs, and Earthquake covers the Steel types that resist both.

Is Tyranitar good in Pokemon GO?

It is one of the most used Rock attackers in the game, and the Dark half gives it a second career against Ghost and Psychic raid bosses. Its Mega form is the reason the line stays relevant between events.

Is there a shiny Tyranitar, and what changes?

Yes. The shiny swaps the olive green hide for a sandy khaki and turns the pale blue belly plate magenta. Nothing else moves: same Rock and Dark typing, same 4x Fighting hole, same Sand Stream.

Which Tyranitar card do collectors mean?

The modern print is Obsidian Flames 66/197, the Double Rare ex, with a 211/197 Ultra Rare full art sitting above it in the same set. Older ex prints exist too: Unseen Forces 111/115 and the Delta Species version in Dragon Frontiers 99/101.
