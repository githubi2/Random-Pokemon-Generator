What is Charmander?

The Lizard Pokemon Charmander entered the national dex at number 4 back in Generation 1. Its Fire type sits on top of a 309 base stat total. It evolves into Charmeleon at level 16, and then into Charizard at level 36. Blaze is the ability it always carries; Solar Power takes the hidden slot. It is one of the classic starter Pokemon of its generation, handed out on the first route. All told the line is 3 stages long: Charmander through Charizard. Its home region is Kanto, the Generation 1 map. Keep those facts in hand — the rest of the page unpacks them.

Its 2x columns are Water, Ground and Rock; the half-damage column runs Fire, Grass, Ice, Bug, Steel and Fairy. Charmander is a Fire type: it takes doubled damage from Water, Ground and Rock and shrugs off Fire, Grass, Ice, Bug, Steel and Fairy. Everything else lands neutral, and that is the whole defensive story. Both lists are short enough to memorize quickly.

What is Charmander weak to?

Water, Ground and Rock, all at 2x damage. On the good half of the sheet, Fire, Grass, Ice, Bug and Steel come in for less than full damage. Everything else lands neutral or weaker. Re-check the card above before a gym, a rival, or any fight that matters.
