What type is Mewtwo?

Mewtwo is a Psychic type. That combination resists Fighting and Psychic and is weak to Bug, Ghost and Dark. Everything else lands neutral, and that is the whole defensive story. Both lists are short enough to memorize quickly.

What is Mewtwo weak to?

Bug, Ghost and Dark, all at 2x damage. On the good half of the sheet, Fighting and Psychic come in for less than full damage. Everything else lands neutral or weaker. Re-check the card above before a gym, a rival, or any fight that matters.

Does Mewtwo evolve?

No — Mewtwo does not evolve at all; there is no earlier or later stage.
