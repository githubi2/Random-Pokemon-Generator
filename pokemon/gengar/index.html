Gengar Pokemon FAQ

What type is Gengar?

Gengar is a Ghost and Poison dual type. That combination resists Grass, Poison, Bug and Fairy and is weak to Ground, Psychic, Ghost and Dark. It is fully immune to Normal and Fighting. Everything else lands neutral, and that is the whole defensive story. Both lists are short enough to memorize quickly.

What is Gengar weak to?

Ground, Psychic, Ghost and Dark, all at 2x damage. Normal and Fighting does nothing at all. On the good half of the sheet, Grass, Poison, Bug and Fairy come in for less than full damage. Everything else lands neutral or weaker. Re-check the card above before a gym, a rival, or any fight that matters.

How does Gengar evolve?

The line runs Gastly → Haunter → Gengar. Every stage of the family keeps the same Ghost/Poison typing. Each jump is permanent, so the timing of each one matters.

What is Gengar's ability?

Its only ability is Cursed Body.
