The Iron Leg Pokemon

Metagross Pokemon: Clear Body, Tough Claws and Four Weaknesses

A computer with legs, on paper

Number 376 is a Steel and Psychic type from Hoenn, 1.6 metres tall and 550 kilograms, and the Pokedex insists it is four Beldum merged into a single body. The design carries through to the stats: this is the slowest kind of Pokemon that still hits first against most walls, because 135 Attack and 130 Defense let it trade with things that are nominally faster.

It is also the only Steel type on this site's Pokedex, which makes it the obvious pick whenever a team needs something that refuses to be chipped down by Fairy, Rock, Ice or Dragon moves.

Four weaknesses instead of a hole

Steel and Psychic together leave exactly four holes: Fire, Ground, Ghost and Dark at double damage. Everything else is neutral or better, including eight resistances, a 0.25x Psychic hit and a total immunity to Poison. There is no 4x weakness anywhere on the card, which is unusual for a Pokemon this heavy on defense.

The catch is that the weaknesses are not rare types. Fire and Ground are on almost every team, and Dark and Ghost moves are common coverage on offensive sets. Metagross survives because 130 Defense and 80 HP turn a 2x hit into something it can eat, not because the typing hides it.

Clear Body is the quiet half of the card

Clear Body blocks every attempt to lower Metagross's stats, and that includes the most common one in the game: Intimidate. A switch-in that drops Attack to -1 does nothing here, which is why Metagross holds its 135 Attack through the entire match against teams built on stat drops.

The hidden ability is Light Metal, which halves its weight and interacts with weight-based moves like Heavy Slam. It is a niche option. Almost every competitive Metagross runs Clear Body, and the Mega form replaces the ability entirely.

Beldum, Metang, Metagross

Three stages, two levels. Beldum evolves into Metang at level 20, and Metang evolves into Metagross at level 45. Beldum is the awkward part: it only learns Take Down, so raising one from scratch means carrying a Pokemon that hurts itself for the first twenty levels.

Mega Evolution is the fourth form and needs the Metagrossite. Mega Metagross keeps Steel and Psychic and moves to 700 total, with 145 Attack, 150 Defense, 110 Special Attack, 110 Special Defense and 110 Speed. Nothing about the typing changes, so the four weaknesses stay exactly where they were.

Tough Claws is what makes the Mega worth it

Mega Metagross swaps Clear Body for Tough Claws, which adds 30% to every move that makes contact. On a Pokemon with 145 Attack that is a large multiplier on the moves it actually uses: Meteor Mash, Zen Headbutt, Bullet Punch and the elemental punches all make contact.

The 40 points of Speed matter just as much. At 110 it outruns the whole unboosted metagame except the fastest threats, which turns Metagross from a Pokemon that trades hits into one that chooses the order of the exchange. The trade is the ability: losing Clear Body means Intimidate works again.

Steel and Psychic, offensively

Steel hits Fairy, Rock and Ice for double damage, and Fairy is the reason the typing has stayed relevant since it was introduced in Gen 6. Psychic hits Fighting and Poison, two types that would otherwise walk through a Steel type, and both STABs together are resisted by very little.

The gap is Steel itself. A Steel type on the other side resists both STABs, and Metagross has to reach for Earthquake or a coverage punch to break through. That single hole is why most sets carry at least one non-STAB move rather than four STABs.

Where Metagross fits on a team

The obvious role is a physical wallbreaker that doubles as a Fairy answer. It switches into Fairy, Rock, Ice, Dragon and Psychic moves without blinking, sets up nothing, and simply hits back with 135 Attack and a STAB that most of the format cannot resist.

The less obvious role is a lead against stat-drop teams. Clear Body makes it immune to the opening Intimidate that most bulky cores rely on, so a Metagross in the first slot forces the opponent to answer it with damage rather than with a debuff.

The Speed 70 question

Base 70 Speed is the number people use against Metagross, and it is a fair criticism: it moves after Garchomp, after most offensive threats, and after anything with a Choice Scarf. But 70 Speed still beats the walls and tanks it is meant to break, and 130 Defense means the hit it takes first is rarely the hit that ends it.

That combination, slow but hard to knock over, is why the Mega form's 110 Speed reads as such a large upgrade. It does not make Metagross fast in absolute terms; it removes the one matchup where the base form loses purely on turn order.

What a Metagross set actually runs

The four moves most sets carry are Meteor Mash, Zen Headbutt, Bullet Punch and one coverage slot. Meteor Mash is the Steel STAB and Metagross's signature, Zen Headbutt is the Psychic STAB with a flinch chance, Bullet Punch is priority that finishes weakened targets, and the fourth slot is usually Earthquake or an elemental punch aimed at a specific wall.

The Mega version changes the priority of those slots rather than the list. With Tough Claws adding 30% and 110 Speed, Bullet Punch becomes a real finisher instead of a cleanup tool, and the coverage slot is often spent on something that handles Steel types, the one thing that resists both STABs.

The base form is better as a bulky answer that never gets its stats lowered, and the Mega is better as a breaker that ends games. Picking between them is a question about the team, not about which one has the bigger numbers.

FAQ

Metagross Pokemon FAQ

What type is Metagross?

Steel and Psychic. That gives it four weaknesses, eight resistances, quarter damage from Psychic moves and a complete immunity to Poison.

What is Metagross weak to?

Fire, Ground, Ghost and Dark each deal double damage. There is no 4x weakness on this Pokemon, which is part of why it is so hard to remove.

How does Metagross evolve?

Beldum becomes Metang at level 20, and Metang becomes Metagross at level 45. Mega Evolution is a separate step and requires the Metagrossite.

What abilities does Metagross have?

Clear Body normally, with Light Metal as the hidden ability. The Mega form replaces both with Tough Claws.

What is Metagross's base stat total?

600 in the base form and 700 as a Mega. The Mega version puts 40 of those points into Speed, which is the change that matters most.

Is Metagross a legendary Pokemon?

No. It is the final stage of the Beldum line, which makes it a pseudo-legendary with the same 600 total as several legendaries and an ordinary evolution method.

What does Clear Body do?

It prevents any attempt to lower Metagross's stats, including Intimidate. Attack stays at 135 no matter how many times the opponent switches a scary Pokemon in.

What does Tough Claws do?

It boosts moves that make contact by 30%. On Mega Metagross that applies to Meteor Mash, Zen Headbutt, Bullet Punch and the elemental punches.

What are Mega Metagross's stats?

145 Attack and 150 Defense are the headline numbers, and 110 Speed finally takes it past most unboosted threats. HP stays at 80, while the special side comes to 105 and 110. That makes 700, with Tough Claws doing the work.

Where do you get Beldum?

Beldum is usually handed out as a gift or found in a rare wild encounter rather than bought, and the source changes between titles. The levels are fixed either way: 20 for Metang, 45 for Metagross.

Is Metagross good in Pokemon GO?

It is one of the strongest Steel attackers in the game, and Meteor Mash is the move that does it. The Mega form gives it a second life as a raid attacker whenever Steel is the answer.

Is there a shiny Metagross, and what changes?

Yes. The blue steel goes silver-grey, and the X across its face turns gold along with the rims around both eyes. The eyes themselves stay red, so the shiny reads as a gold mask on a silver body.

Which Metagross card is the expensive one?

Steven's Metagross ex, in two prints: Destined Rivals 145/182 is the Double Rare, and Ascended Heroes 289/217 is the Special Illustration Rare. Metagross also has an Illustration Rare in Temporal Forces, 178/162.
