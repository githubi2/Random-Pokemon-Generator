The Spike Ball Pokemon

Togepi Pokemon: The Egg, The Friendship, The Stone

An egg with feet, and a small one

Number 175 is 0.3 metres tall and weighs 1.5 kilograms, which makes it one of the smallest Pokemon in the Pokedex. It has been a Fairy type since Gen 6 and was a Normal type before that, a change that rewrote its entire weakness list overnight.

The Pokedex calls it the Spike Ball Pokemon and the design is exactly that: a round shell covered in red and blue triangles. The shell is also the reason it cannot fight back properly. Attack 20 and Speed 20 are not numbers that win exchanges.

How to evolve Togepi

Friendship, not levels. Togepi evolves into Togetic when it levels up while its friendship is high, and there is no level requirement attached. The threshold most friendship evolutions use is 220 out of a possible 255, so the requirement is real and the game never shows you the number.

The fastest route is dull but reliable: keep it in the party, walk with it, give it the Soothe Bell, avoid letting it faint, and feed it the berries that raise friendship. Every level-up after that point is a coin flip only because you cannot see the counter, not because the mechanic is random.

Togetic needs a stone, and that is a Gen 4 change

Togetic evolves into Togekiss with a Shiny Stone. That step did not exist until Gen 4, which means a Togepi raised in Gold or Silver could never reach its final form, no matter how much friendship it accumulated.

So the full chain is three stages with two completely different mechanics: friendship for the first step, an item for the second. Anyone searching for a Togepi evolution chart is really asking about both, and the stone is the part that gets missed.

245 total, and what that number means

The two defensives top out at 65 each, with 35 HP and 40 Special Attack in support and 20 in both Attack and Speed, for 245 total. Those are baby-Pokemon numbers by design, and they come with the baby-Pokemon rule: Togepi cannot breed, so the only way to get another one is an egg from somewhere else.

Defense and Special Defense at 65 are the only stats that are not actively bad, which tells you what the design intended. Togepi is supposed to survive long enough to evolve, not to fight.

Serene Grace is the ability people remember

Togepi can have Hustle, Serene Grace or the hidden Super Luck. Hustle raises Attack by 50% and cuts accuracy by 20%, which is a bad trade on a Pokemon with 20 Attack. Super Luck raises the critical hit rate. Serene Grace doubles the chance of every secondary effect on its moves.

Serene Grace is the one that matters, and not because of anything Togepi does. It is the ability that makes Togekiss work: doubled flinch chances on Air Slash is the entire strategy, and it is inherited straight from the egg.

Fairy typing, and what it changed

Before Gen 6, Togepi was a Normal type with one weakness to Fighting. After the retcon it is a Fairy type with weaknesses to Poison and Steel, resistances to Fighting, Bug and Dark, and a complete immunity to Dragon.

The switch made the line better, not worse. Dragon immunity is a rare and valuable thing, and it applies to every stage of the chain, which is part of why Togekiss stayed relevant through several generations of metagame shifts.

Where Togepi actually comes from

In Gold, Silver and their remakes, a Togepi egg is handed to the player by Mr. Pokemon early in the game, before the first gym badge. It is one of the few Pokemon in the series that is given rather than caught, and the egg hatches after a set number of steps like any other.

Later titles keep the pattern and move the giver around. If you are looking for a Togepi in a specific game, the answer is almost always an NPC with an egg rather than a patch of grass.

What Togekiss does that Togepi cannot

Togekiss goes to 120 Special Attack and 115 Special Defense, with 95 Defense, 85 HP, 80 Speed and 50 Attack filling the rest: 545 total, and it keeps Serene Grace. Air Slash with a doubled flinch chance is the move the whole build exists for, and the bulk is enough to keep using it.

None of that is available to Togepi, and none of it is available to Togetic either. The line only becomes a real competitive Pokemon at the final stage, which is the honest answer to whether Togepi is worth raising: yes, if you intend to finish the chain.

The details that actually speed up friendship

Four things move the counter, and they stack. Walking with the Pokemon in the party adds a point every few hundred steps, the Soothe Bell multiplies every gain, and a Pokemon in a Luxury Ball gains friendship faster than one in any other ball.

What sets it back matters just as much. Fainting costs friendship, so a Togepi that keeps getting knocked out is actively going backwards. The berries that lower effort values (Pomeg, Kelpsy, Qualot and the rest) raise friendship instead, which makes them the fastest item shortcut outside of battle-healing loops.

None of this is visible anywhere in the game. There is no friendship meter on the summary screen until the later titles added a rough heart indicator, so the honest advice is to stack the multipliers and stop worrying about the exact number.

FAQ

Togepi Pokemon FAQ

What type is Togepi?

Fairy. It was a Normal type until Gen 6, when the Fairy type was introduced and Togepi was moved over with the rest of the retconned species.

What is Togepi weak to?

Poison and Steel each deal double damage. Fighting, Bug and Dark are halved, and Dragon does nothing at all.

How does Togepi evolve?

By friendship. Level it up while its friendship is high and it becomes Togetic. There is no level requirement, so the trigger is the bond rather than the experience.

How do you evolve Togepi fast?

Stack the multipliers rather than grinding: Soothe Bell held, a Luxury Ball capture, and a lot of steps. Fainting costs friendship, so switching out early is worth more than one extra battle.

Does Togepi evolve with a stone?

Not Togepi. Togetic is the stage that needs a Shiny Stone, and that step only exists from Gen 4 onward.

What abilities does Togepi have?

Hustle and Serene Grace, with Super Luck as the hidden ability. Serene Grace is the one worth keeping because it carries over to Togekiss.

What is Togepi's base stat total?

245. Defense and Special Defense at 65 are the only respectable numbers, which is normal for a baby Pokemon that is meant to be raised rather than used.

Is Togepi good in battle?

No. 20 Attack and 20 Speed do not win fights. Togekiss is the competitive stage of the line, and it is a genuinely strong one.

Can Togepi breed?

No. Baby Pokemon cannot produce eggs, so the only way to obtain another Togepi is to receive or hatch an egg from an NPC or a trade.

What generation is Togepi from?

Gen 2. It debuted in Gold and Silver as the Pokemon that hatches from the egg Mr. Pokemon gives the player early in the story.

What are Togekiss's stats?

120 Special Attack and 115 Special Defense carry the line, with 95 Defense, 85 HP, 80 Speed and 50 Attack around them: 545 total, and Serene Grace doubles the flinch chances.

Is there a shiny Togepi, and what changes?

Yes. The cream shell picks up a pink tint and the triangles swap colours: the red ones turn blue and the blue ones turn red. The face and the shape stay identical, so the tell is the pattern rather than the body.
