Rayquaza Pokemon: Stats, Weaknesses and Ability Notes

Meet Rayquaza, the Sky High Pokemon

Number 384 belongs to Hoenn and Generation 3, and the dex calls it the Sky High Pokemon. Dragon and Flying is the typing, Air Lock is the ability, and there is no evolution stage attached to it in either direction. Like the rest of its trio, it turns up in the story rather than through a distribution event.

The stat line is the interesting part. 150 Attack and 150 Special Attack side by side is rare even among the 680-total crowd, most of which pick one side and commit. Rayquaza can be built either way, and a mixed set is genuinely viable rather than a compromise.

Air Lock switches off the weather

Air Lock cancels every weather effect while Rayquaza is on the field: rain, harsh sunlight, sandstorms, hail, the lot. In a generation whose headline battles revolve around exactly those conditions, that is not a small ability. It is the reason the Pokemon reads as an arbiter rather than a participant.

The practical effect is broad. Sandstorm chip damage stops accumulating, rain stops boosting Water attacks, weather-based speed abilities stop firing, and healing tied to weather goes quiet. Anyone whose plan depends on a forecast has to make a new plan as soon as this Pokemon comes out.

Reading the stat line

Two columns carry the spread: 150 Attack and 150 Special Attack. Behind them sit 105 HP, 95 Speed and 90 in both defensive stats. That is a fast, hard-hitting Pokemon with enough raw health to take one solid hit, which is usually all a legendary attacker needs.

The 90s are the trade-off. Against the defensive titans of the dex it is the least sturdy member of the 680 club, and bulkier attackers can outlast it if a fight goes long. Rayquaza wins by ending battles early rather than by absorbing them.

Weaknesses and resistances in one pass

The weakness column runs four deep: Ice, Rock, Dragon and Fairy. On the other side sit five resistances, Fire, Water, Grass, Fighting and Bug, plus a clean immunity to Ground handed over by the Flying typing. Everything left in the chart comes in neutral.

Ice is the answer opponents reach for first, and it is easy to see why: it catches both types at once. Dragon and Fairy moves turn up often enough on strong attackers that they are worth tracking too, while a Ground-type opponent is free ground for a switch.

The mega form changes the total

Rayquaza has a mega evolution, and it works differently from the rest. Instead of holding a Mega Stone, it needs to know the move Dragon Ascent. That quirk is why the form is often described as an upgrade rather than a transformation item.

The result is the highest base stat total in the series. That is the reason the big-form pages in the suite are worth a look even for a Pokemon that does not need help: the ceiling here is higher than anything else in the dex reaches.

Where it stands in the Hoenn trio

Kyogre brings the rain, Groudon brings the sun, and Rayquaza is the one that ends the argument. The lore and the mechanics line up neatly here: an ability that switches off weather is the cleanest possible answer to two Pokemon whose entire threat profile depends on it.

That is also what makes it the most flexible of the three to use. Kyogre and Groudon want a specific condition before they function at their best. Rayquaza wants the opposite: a battlefield with nothing extra on it, where two 150s and a 95 Speed stat can simply do the work.

What to expect in a fight

Rayquaza plays as a fast breaker. It comes in, hits harder than almost anything on the field, and leaves before the accumulated damage catches up with it. The 90s in Defence and Special Defence make that timing matter: this is not a Pokemon that wins a long exchange.

Against teams built around weather, the ability alone can be decisive. Against everything else, it comes down to the same question every offensive legendary faces: can the opponent survive two hits, and can they land one that hurts before the second arrives.

FAQ

Rayquaza Pokemon FAQ

What type is Rayquaza?

Rayquaza is a Dragon and Flying type. That gives it four weaknesses, five resistances and one immunity to Ground, and it is the same combination Dragonite carries.

What is Rayquaza weak to?

Ice, Rock, Dragon and Fairy all land for double damage. Ice is the sharpest of the four because it hits both halves of the typing, and Ground does nothing to it at all.

Does Rayquaza evolve?

No. Rayquaza has no evolution before it and none after it, so the line is a single stage that never changes.

What is Rayquaza's ability?

Air Lock. While Rayquaza is on the field, every weather effect is cancelled, which covers rain and sun boosts, sandstorm and hail damage, and the abilities that depend on each of them.

What does the Rayquaza evolution chart look like?

There is no chart, because nothing in the line changes stage. The only form change attached to it is a mega evolution, which needs the move Dragon Ascent rather than an item.

Is Rayquaza a legendary Pokemon?

Rayquaza is a legendary, part of the Hoenn trio with Kyogre and Groudon. It is met during the story rather than handed out at an event, which separates it from the mythical group.
