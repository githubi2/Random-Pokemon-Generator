What is Eevee?

The Evolution Pokemon Eevee entered the national dex at number 133 back in Generation 1. It carries the Normal type and a 325 base stat total. It heads a branched family of 8 evolutions: Vaporeon, Jolteon, Flareon, Espeon, Umbreon, Leafeon, Glaceon and Sylveon. Run Away and Adaptability are its regular abilities, and Anticipation takes the hidden slot. The family counts nine members in total — Eevee and its eight branches, with no branch leading into another. Its home region is Kanto, the Generation 1 map. FAQ

Eevee Pokemon FAQ

What type is Eevee?

Eevee is a Normal type. It takes doubled damage from Fighting, and nothing bounces off it at half power. It is fully immune to Ghost. Everything else lands neutral, and that is the whole defensive story. Both lists are short enough to memorize quickly.

What is Eevee weak to?

Fighting, all at 2x damage. Ghost does nothing at all. With no resist list, positioning does the work a resists column normally would. Everything else lands neutral or weaker. Re-check the card above before a gym, a rival, or any fight that matters.

How does Eevee evolve?

Eevee branches rather than chains: one Eevee becomes exactly one of eight evolutions. Stones handle Vaporeon, Jolteon and Flareon; friendship at the right time of day gives Espeon or Umbreon; a mossy or icy rock adds Leafeon and Glaceon; Sylveon comes from affection. None of the eight evolves any further, and the typing changes with the branch.

What abilities does Eevee have?

Run Away and Adaptability come standard, and Anticipation is the rare hidden pick. Only one ability slot is ever active at a time. Hidden abilities usually need special methods to obtain in the games. The choice of build sits right on top of that ability line.

What does the Eevee evolution chart look like?

It reads as a fan, not a line: one Eevee on the left, eight arrows out, and a trigger beside each arrow — three stones, two friendships, two location rocks, and Sylveon's affection. Reading it top to bottom still maps any playthrough of the family.

What is Eevee best at?

Its Sp. Def of 65 is the standout of that 325 total, with the rest of the spread built to back it up. That is the number teammates get chosen around.
