Ceruledge

Ceruledge is the sword half of the Charcadet split: Fire and Ghost, 125 Attack, and a signature move that turns damage into healing. The typing gives it two immunities that matter more than any resistance, and five weaknesses that make it a Pokemon you have to place carefully.

The Fire Blades Pokemon

Ceruledge Pokemon: Bitter Blade, Weak Armor and Two Immunities

One Charcadet, two armors

Charcadet is a small Fire type that evolves into one of two completely different Pokemon depending on the item used on it. The Malicious Armor produces Ceruledge, the Auspicious Armor produces Armarouge, and the choice is permanent once it is made.

The split is not cosmetic. Ceruledge is physical with 125 Attack and a Ghost typing. Armarouge is special with 125 Special Attack and a Psychic typing. Same 525 total, opposite ways of using it, and no way to reverse the decision.

Fire and Ghost, and why the typing is worth it

Fire and Ghost is one of the rarest pairings in the game, and the reason teams use it is the defensive side rather than the offensive one. Normal moves cannot touch a Ghost type, and Fighting moves cannot either, so two of the most common physical attacking types in the game simply do nothing.

The cost is real: Water, Ground, Rock, Ghost and Dark all hit for double damage. Five weaknesses on a Pokemon with 75 HP and 80 Defense means it does not want to take a neutral hit either, let alone a super effective one.

Bitter Blade is the whole reason it works

Bitter Blade is Ceruledge's signature move: a physical Fire attack that heals the user for half the damage it deals. On a Pokemon with 125 Attack, that converts every turn into a trade it wins, which is unusual for something this fragile.

The healing is not a gimmick attached to a weak move either. It is a STAB Fire attack on an offensive stat of 125, so the same move that keeps Ceruledge alive is also the move that removes the opponent's Pokemon.

Weak Armor, and the trade it offers

Weak Armor lowers Defense by one stage and raises Speed by two every time the Pokemon is hit by a physical move. Since Gen 8 that has been two stages rather than one, which turns two physical hits into a doubled Speed stat.

That is exactly what a Pokemon with 85 Speed and 125 Attack wants. It also accelerates the process of dying, because the Defense drop stacks with the damage. Ceruledge either sweeps after taking one hit or falls over trying, and both outcomes happen often.

525 total: where the points went

75 HP, 125 Attack, 80 Defense, 60 Special Attack, 100 Special Defense and 85 Speed. The design is clear from the numbers: everything went into Attack, with Special Defense as the surprise stat at 100 and Special Attack deliberately abandoned at 60.

That spread means Ceruledge has exactly one job and does not pretend otherwise. It is a physical breaker with a healing STAB, and it has no business being on a team that needs a special attacker.

What beats Ceruledge, and how

Anything with Water, Ground or Rock coverage removes it quickly, and Dark and Ghost moves do the same through its own typing. Physically bulky Pokemon that can take a Bitter Blade and answer with a neutral hit also work, because the healing is proportional to damage dealt.

The counterplay is straightforward enough that good players plan for it: switch something in that resists Fire, force the Weak Armor trade, and hit the lowered Defense. Ceruledge wins the games where the opponent cannot do that.

Ceruledge or Armarouge

Both are 525 total and both have Flash Fire with Weak Armor as the hidden option. The difference is which stat got the 125: Ceruledge is physical and Armarouge is special, so the two fill opposite roles on a team.

Ceruledge is generally the easier one to build around, because physical Fire attackers with healing are rare and the Ghost typing adds immunities. Armarouge is the better pick when the team already has physical attackers and needs a special breaker.

Where Charcadet comes from

Charcadet is found in the Paldea region, and the armor it needs is bought with a trade rather than with money. In Zapapico an NPC takes ten Sinistea Chips for the Malicious Armor, and ten Bronzor Fragments for the Auspicious Armor.

That means the evolution is gated behind a scavenger hunt rather than a level. It is also why the two forms are version-flavoured: Violet leans toward Ceruledge's armor, Scarlet toward Armarouge's.

Swords Dance is the move that changes the Pokemon

At +0, Ceruledge is a decent breaker that heals. At +2, it is a Pokemon that removes two or three members of a team in sequence, because 125 Attack doubled clears almost every neutral target in one hit and Bitter Blade heals back whatever it took getting there.

The problem is the turn it costs. Swords Dance on a Pokemon with 75 HP and 80 Defense means taking a hit before doing anything, and five weaknesses mean that hit is often doubled. Weak Armor partially pays for it by raising Speed, so the sequence that works is: take one physical hit, boost, then move first for the rest of the game.

That sequence is why Ceruledge sets are built around surviving exactly one turn rather than around bulk. Focus Sash, a bulky teammate to switch into, or a free turn created by a switch are all ways of buying the same thing.

Shiny Ceruledge: blue fire, same sword

The shiny version of Ceruledge changes one thing and changes it well. The armour is the same dark navy, the blades are the same shape, and the only difference is colour: the flame on its head and the glow along the swords go from pink-purple to blue.

That matters more than it sounds, because the flame is the most visible part of the silhouette in a battle animation. In a double battle or a crowded raid, the blue crest is the tell, not the body.

What does not change is everything that makes the Pokemon work. 525 total, 125 Attack, Weak Armor as the hidden ability and Bitter Blade healing half its damage. The shiny is a coat of paint on the same glass cannon.

FAQ

Ceruledge Pokemon FAQ

What type is Ceruledge?

Fire and Ghost. That pairing gives it immunities to Normal and Fighting, and five weaknesses at double damage.

What is Ceruledge weak to?

Water, Ground, Rock, Ghost and Dark each deal double damage. Fire, Grass, Ice, Poison, Bug, Steel and Fairy are all halved instead.

How does Ceruledge evolve?

Charcadet evolves into Ceruledge when a Malicious Armor is used on it. The alternate path, the Auspicious Armor, produces Armarouge instead.

What abilities does Ceruledge have?

Flash Fire normally, with Weak Armor as the hidden ability. Competitive sets almost always want Weak Armor for the Speed boost.

What is Ceruledge's base stat total?

525. Attack is 125, Special Defense 100, and Special Attack is only 60, which tells you exactly what the Pokemon is for.

What does Bitter Blade do?

It is a physical Fire attack that heals Ceruledge for half of the damage it deals. It is both the STAB and the recovery, which is why the Pokemon works at all.

How does Weak Armor work?

Taking a physical hit lowers Defense by one stage and raises Speed by two, which has been the case since Gen 8. It stacks, so repeat hits keep accelerating it.

Is Ceruledge better than Armarouge?

It depends on the team. Ceruledge is the physical attacker with a healing STAB, Armarouge is the special attacker with a Psychic typing. Both total 525.

Where do you find Charcadet?

In Paldea. The armor is obtained in Zapapico by trading ten Sinistea Chips for the Malicious Armor, or ten Bronzor Fragments for the Auspicious Armor.

What moves does Ceruledge run?

Bitter Blade, Shadow Claw, Swords Dance and usually a coverage slot like Close Combat. The first two are the STABs and Swords Dance is what turns 125 Attack into a sweep.

Is Ceruledge good competitively?

It is a solid physical breaker in the tiers where it is legal, mostly because Bitter Blade makes it hard to trade with. Its fragility against Water, Ground and Rock is the limit.

Is there a shiny Ceruledge, and what changes?

Yes. The armour stays dark navy, but the flame on its head turns blue instead of pink-purple, and the glow running along both blades changes with it. Everything on the card stays the same: same 525 total, same Weak Armor, same Bitter Blade.

Which Ceruledge cards are people searching for?

Ceruledge ex is Surging Sparks 36/191, the Double Rare. The Illustration Rare from the same set is 197/191, which is the number behind most of the card-number searches, and Paldean Fates has a Shiny Rare at 162/91.
