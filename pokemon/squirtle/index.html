Squirtle Pokemon FAQ

What type is Squirtle?

Squirtle is a Water type: it takes doubled damage from Electric and Grass and shrugs off Fire, Water, Ice and Steel. Everything else lands neutral, and that is the whole defensive story. Both lists are short enough to memorize quickly.

What is Squirtle weak to?

Electric and Grass, all at 2x damage. On the good half of the sheet, Fire, Water, Ice and Steel come in for less than full damage. Everything else lands neutral or weaker. Re-check the card above before a gym, a rival, or any fight that matters.

How does Squirtle evolve?

The line runs Squirtle → Wartortle → Blastoise. Every stage of the family keeps the same Water typing. Each jump is permanent, so the timing of each one matters.

What abilities does Squirtle have?

Torrent is the ability it always carries; Rain Dish takes the hidden slot. Only one ability slot is ever active at a time. Hidden abilities usually need special methods to obtain in the games. The choice of build sits right on top of that ability line.
