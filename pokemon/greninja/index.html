What type is Greninja?

Greninja is a Water and Dark dual type. That combination resists Fire, Water, Ice, Ghost, Dark and Steel and is weak to Electric, Grass, Fighting, Bug and Fairy. It is fully immune to Psychic. Everything else lands neutral, and that is the whole defensive story. Both lists are short enough to memorize quickly.

What is Greninja weak to?
