How it works

How Pokemon Search Works

One box, all 1,025 species

The search bar queries the same national dex every other tool on this site uses: all 1,025 species from Generation 1 through 9, plus regional and special forms. Type the full name or just a fragment — "pika" finds Pikachu and Raichu instantly — and the results update as you type. Every row shows the official artwork, national dex number, types, generation and base stat total, so you can identify a mystery Pokemon without leaving the page.

Partial names are matched against both the species slug and its displayed form, so search stays forgiving across trades, translations and nickname-less dex browsing.

Jump from search to tools

Every result opens the Pokemon in the Random Pokemon Generator with one click, where the full detail card waits: base stats, abilities, type coverage, evolution chain and a shiny variant. From there the rest of the suite is one tap away — check what beats it on the Pokemon Type Chart, verify its natures with the Pokemon Nature Chart, or find its IVs with the Pokemon IV Calculator.

Search is also the entry point for browsing: want a Water-type squad? The generator's type filter does that on the Pokemon Team Picker page, and if you only need one pick, the Random Pokemon Picker rolls it.

Built for quick lookups

Whether you are checking a trade on your phone or building a team at the computer, search keeps the flow short: type, read, open. The results stay local — nothing is uploaded, no account, no tracking. It is the same philosophy as the Who's That Pokemon guessing game: get you to the Pokemon you are thinking about in the fewest possible clicks.

Search is the door to the whole suite. From a result you can jump into the Nuzlocke Team Builder, the Pokemon Wheel Spinner, or a Pokemon Smash or Pass verdict for that exact Pokemon. The Random Mega Pokemon Generator and Random Shiny Pokemon Generator cover the flair picks, Pokemon Shiny Odds plans the real hunts, and the Random Pokemon Name Generator names the catch. Finished? Turn it into a card with the Pokemon Card Generator and Pokemon Trainer Card Generator, and read the deeper guides on the Blog.

Why a local search beats a list

The national dex is more than 1,000 entries long — a searchable index is the difference between finding a Pokemon in two seconds and scrolling forever. Because the same dataset powers every tool on this site, the Pokemon you find in search is the exact same Pokemon the generator rolls, the team picker slots, and the type chart evaluates. No drift between pages, no stale dex numbers: search is a window into one consistent database.

For competitive work the flow is even better. Search for Garchomp, open it in the generator, and the detail card lists its base stats, abilities and type coverage in one screen; the Random Pokemon Picker and Pokemon Team Picker pages rest on that same data for their random rolls and matchup maps. Learn the matchup from the Pokemon Type Chart, verify the nature with the Pokemon Nature Chart, then test the result with the Pokemon IV Calculator — every step references the same dex entry you found by name.

Search also quietly doubles as a dex browser while you play. Running a Nuzlocke and need to recall what a route's encounter can evolve into? Search the name, then roll the evolved form's matchups on the type chart. Drafting a league and comparing two candidates? Search each name, note their base stats side by side, and let the team picker decide. Hunters can pair search with Pokemon Shiny Odds for the exact rate, and collectors can flip any result into artwork with the Pokemon Card Generator. The dex you are searching is the same dex every battle starts from.

FAQ

Pokemon Search FAQ

How does Pokemon Search work?

Type any Pokemon name or part of one — the search filters the full national dex of 1,025 species instantly and shows each match with its types, generation and base stat total. Clicking a result opens that Pokemon in the random generator.

What Pokemon are searchable?

Every species from Generation 1 through 9 — 1,025 Pokemon plus regional and special forms. Data comes from PokeAPI, the same source used by every tool on this site.

Does search work for nicknames or abilities?

This page searches by species name only. For competitive study, open the Pokemon in the generator via the result link, then use the detail card to browse its abilities, stats, type coverage and evolution chain.
