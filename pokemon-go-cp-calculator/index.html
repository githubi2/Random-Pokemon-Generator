    How the Pokemon GO CP Calculator works

    Every Pokemon in GO carries three stats: base Attack, base Defense and base Stamina. Your IVs add 0 to 15 to each of the three, the Pokemon's level sets a multiplier called CPM, and the game combines them like this:

    CP = floor( (Attack + IV) x √(Defense + IV) x √(Stamina + IV) x CPM2 / 10 )

    Attack enters the formula unmultiplied while Defense and Stamina sit inside square roots, which is the reason CP leans so heavily toward attackers. A Mewtwo with 0/0/0 IVs at level 40 comes out at 3,697; the same Mewtwo with 15/15/15 comes out at 4,178. Same species, same level, 481 CP apart, and every point of that gap is IV.

    Nothing about CP is random and nothing is hidden. If you know the species, the IVs and the level, this calculator gives you the exact number the game will display, because it runs the same arithmetic the client does.

    What IVs actually do to the number

    IVs are worth less than most players expect and the gap is easy to see. Across the whole dex, a perfect 15/15/15 set adds roughly 8 to 10 percent to CP compared with 0/0/0, because 15 points spread over base stats in the 100 to 300 range is a small slice.

    Level is the bigger lever, and by a wide margin. Powering the same Mewtwo from level 20 to level 40 more than doubles its CP, while going from a 0/0/0 spread to a perfect one adds a few hundred. That is why a level 35 with bad IVs usually beats a level 20 with perfect ones in raw CP.

    The one place IVs decide fights is capped PvP, where both Pokemon sit at the CP ceiling and the stat spread is all that is left to compare. There, a 0/15/15 spread is often better than 15/15/15, because low Attack lets you reach a higher level under the same cap and the extra bulk wins the mirror.

    Level, CPM, and why there are two caps

    Level runs from 1 to 50 in half steps, and each step carries its own multiplier. The jump from level 1 (CPM 0.094) to level 40 (0.7903) is the whole power-up grind; the last ten levels add another 6 percent or so, which is why XL candy stretches a Pokemon rather than transforming it.

    Two rules are worth knowing because they change the answer people expect. A best buddy gets one extra level on top of whatever you have powered it to, and a Pokemon caught with weather boost arrives five levels above its normal catch level (level 25 instead of 20 for raids, 35 instead of 30 in the wild).

    Bulbasaur shows the range: 1,115 CP at level 40 with perfect IVs, 1,260 at level 50. The species does not change; only the multiplier does.

    What happens to CP when it evolves

    Evolution keeps the two things you cannot change, level and IVs, and swaps the base stats underneath them. So the CP after evolving is simply the new species' CP at your current level with your current IVs, which is why the jump looks dramatic on a three-stage line and modest on a two-stage one.

    One consequence catches people out in PvP. Evolving a Pokemon that sits just under 1,500 CP usually pushes it over the Great League cap, and there is no way back down. If the final evolution is the one you want for Great League, the level you should stop at belongs to that species, not the one you are holding now.

    Where CP lies: the league caps

    Great League stops at 1,500 CP and Ultra League at 2,500, so the strongest Pokemon for those formats is the one that squeezes the most stats under the line, not the one with the biggest number. Take a perfect Mewtwo: it hits 1,500 territory at level 12.5 with 1,492 CP, and one half-level more takes it out of Great League entirely. In Ultra League the same Pokemon peaks at level 20.5 and 2,447 CP.

    That is why a level 40 Pokemon you powered up for raids can be useless in Great League: it blew past the cap long ago and there is no way to power a Pokemon down. Before you invest, run the numbers for the league you actually play.

    Pokemon GO CP Calculator FAQ

    How is CP calculated in Pokemon GO?

    CP comes from three hidden stats and the Pokemon's level. Each species has a base Attack, Defense and Stamina; your IVs add 0 to 15 on top of each; then the whole thing is multiplied by the level multiplier squared and divided by ten. The game always rounds down and never shows less than 10.

    Does a high CP mean a better Pokemon?

    Not by itself. CP weighs Attack most heavily, so a high-Attack species with poor bulk can out-CP a sturdier one that wins more fights. Two Pokemon at the same CP can also have very different IV spreads, and in capped PvP the lower-CP one is often the better pick.

    What is the highest CP in Pokemon GO?

    Among the species this calculator covers, Slaking tops out at 5,010 at level 50 with perfect IVs. The crowned forms go higher still: Zacian's crowned form reaches 5,629 and Zamazenta's crowned form 5,311, and neither needs a level cap to do it.

    What CP is a perfect Mewtwo?

    A 15/15/15 Mewtwo is 4,178 at level 40 and 4,724 once it reaches level 50. At the level-25 catch point a perfect one shows 2,984, while a 0/0/0 shows 2,641, which is the whole spread IVs can open up on that species.

    Why is my level 40 CP lower than someone else's?

    Same species and same level, different IVs. The gap is biggest on bulky species: Mewtwo ranges from 3,697 to 4,178 at level 40, a 481 CP spread from IVs alone.

    What level should I power up to for Great League?

    Stop at the highest level that keeps the CP at or under 1,500. For a perfect Mewtwo that is level 12.5 at 1,492 CP; one more half-level pushes it over the cap and out of the league.

    Does evolving change CP?

    Yes, and by a lot. Evolution keeps your level and IVs but swaps in the new species' base stats, so the CP recalculates to whatever that species would be at your level. A 500 CP starter is rarely still 500 after it evolves.

    What is the highest CP you can catch in the wild?

    Wild Pokemon appear at level 1 to 30, or up to 35 when the weather boosts their type. Raid catches sit at level 20, or 25 with weather boost, which is why a freshly caught legendary already lands near 2,984 with perfect IVs.

    Do IVs matter more than level?

    Level moves CP far more than IVs do at the low end: going from level 20 to 40 nearly doubles CP, while a perfect set of IVs adds roughly 8 to 10 percent. IVs only become the deciding factor when two Pokemon are already at the same level.

    Can CP go over 4000?

    Only for a short list of species. This calculator's level 50 table has 62 species above 4,000 with perfect IVs, led by Slaking at 5,010.
