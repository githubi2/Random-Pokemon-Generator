        How it works

        How to Run a Nuzlocke with This Generator

        The classic Nuzlocke rules

        A Nuzlocke run works because of three simple self-imposed rules: you may only catch the first Pokémon you see in each area, a fainted Pokémon is permanently gone, and every catch gets a nickname. That turns an ordinary playthrough into a survival run where one bad encounter can end a teammate for good. Many players add optional rules too — no items in battle, no switching, no healing outside Poké Centers — but the first-catch rule is the heart of it. This Nuzlocke generator runs on the same Gen 1–9 dataset as our main random Pokemon generator.

        Start with a six-Pokémon starter squad

        Pick Starter Team mode, choose the generation that matches your game, and roll. You get six random Pokémon from that generation — legendaries and mythicals are banned by default, so the squad is always a legal Nuzlocke team. Playing Kanto? Select Gen 1 and the pool becomes the original 151. Playing Paldea? Select Gen 9 and roll from the newest roster. Need extra dimensions like type filters, base stat ranges, or shiny mode? The full Pokemon team builder on our home page has them all.

        Roll one encounter per route

        The heart of a Nuzlocke is the first-encounter rule. Switch to First Encounter mode and roll once every time you enter a new route: whichever Pokémon appears is your one and only catch for that area. No rerolls, no exceptions — that is the rule. If you get a duplicate or a Pokémon you already have, stiff upper lip, that is part of the challenge.

        Replace the fallen

        When a member of your team faints, it is gone forever — but the run is not over. Switch to Death Replacement mode, roll a single replacement from the same generation pool, and carry on. The generator keeps the same region locked, so your replacement always fits the game you are playing. Some players roll a replacement that is one evolution stage lower on purpose, for the extra challenge.

        Tune the ruleset to your challenge

        Turn on Unevolved Only to force yourself to roll first-stage Pokémon and raise the difficulty, or use a Kaizo-style ruleset where legendaries are allowed but items are not. This Nuzlocke generator's rule toggles let you dial the challenge from casual to hardcore: keep legendaries banned for a classic run, allow them for a power fantasy, or roll Only-mythical for a themed challenge.

        Which generation should you Nuzlocke?

        Every generation offers a different Nuzlocke flavor. Gen 1 Kanto is the classic — 151 Pokémon, tight routes, and legendary difficulty spikes at the Elite Four. Gen 2 Johto adds two regions and the hardest gym lead in the series. Gen 4 Sinnoh is a legendary for hardcore rulesets, with brutal coverage checks and high-level wilds. Gen 5 Unova forces a fresh team on you after the story. Gen 9 Paldea is the friendliest starting point for a first Nuzlocke, with open-world routes full of easy catches. Whichever you pick, lock the matching generation here and the pool will always fit your region.

        Build, export, and remember every run

        Beyond random rolls, the generator works as a real Nuzlocke manager. Search any of the 1,025 Pokémon and add them to your team manually when you catch a new encounter by hand. Export your current squad to Pokémon Showdown format with one click — name, ability and level, ready to paste into any simulator or forum post. And when a teammate falls, mark it as fallen: the graveyard keeps an honest record of every Pokémon you lose, with a one-click revive if a Nuzlocke rule says your run continues. It is the full loop: roll, build, test, and mourn. Between runs, judge the whole roster with the Pokemon smash or pass game, or spin the random Pokemon generator wheel for a fate-picked starter.

        Share your run as you go

        Every roll creates a share link that encodes the mode, generation, rules, and the exact Pokémon rolled — shinies included if you get lucky. Post it in your Nuzlocke community, on Discord, or to friends next to your badge progress. They can open the exact same encounter, or re-roll from your ruleset and compare runs side by side.

        FAQ

        Nuzlocke Generator & Team Builder FAQ

        What is a Nuzlocke run?

        A Nuzlocke is a self-imposed Pokemon challenge run: you can only catch the first Pokemon you see in each area, any Pokemon that faints is permanently lost (released or boxed), and you must nickname every catch. It turns the main story into a survival run where every encounter matters.

        How do I start a Nuzlocke with this generator?

        Pick the Starter Team mode, choose your generation (Gen 1 for Kanto, Gen 9 for Paldea, and so on), keep Legendary/Mythical banned, and hit Roll. You get a six-Pokemon starter squad to begin the run.

        What is the First Encounter mode?

        Strict Nuzlocke rules allow only one catch per route. First Encounter mode rolls a single random Pokemon per click — use it when you enter a new route, and whatever the generator deals you is your one and only catch for that area.

        What happens when a Pokemon faints in my run?

        Under Nuzlocke rules a fainted Pokemon is gone for good. Switch to Death Replacement mode, roll one replacement from the same generation pool, and keep the run going — same rules, fresh luck.

        Are legendaries and mythicals included?

        By default this Nuzlocke generator excludes legendaries and mythicals, matching most Nuzlocke rulesets. You can toggle either rule off, or roll Only mode for themed challenge runs.

        Can I share my Nuzlocke rolls?

        Yes. Every roll produces a share link that encodes the mode, generation, rules, and the exact Pokemon rolled — post it in your Nuzlocke community or to friends so they can see (or re-roll) the same encounter.
