Reference

Base Shiny Odds by Generation

Shiny Pokemon debuted in Generation II, and the base rate only changed once in series history. Gold, Silver and every game through Black 2 and White 2 rolled 1 in 8,192; X and Y doubled everyone's luck to 1 in 4,096, and every mainline game since — including Legends Arceus, Let's Go and Scarlet & Violet — starts from that same 1 in 4,096 base before boosts.

Shiny Odds Boost Methods

Every boost in the series works the same way under the hood: the game rolls the shiny check extra times per encounter and keeps the best result. More rolls, better odds. Game by game

Shiny Odds by Game

Pokemon Legends: Arceus shiny odds

Legends Arceus starts at the modern 1 in 4,096 base, then layers research bonuses on top: raising a species' Pokedex research to level 10 moves it to 1 in 2,048, and a perfect research page improves that to 1 in 1,024. The Shiny Charm — earned by reaching research level 10 on every species — adds three more rolls on its own, and all of it stacks. The real stars are mass outbreaks: an outbreak alone jumps to roughly 1 in 158, and a perfect-dex outbreak with the Charm active reaches 32 rolls per spawn, the famous 1 in 128 — the best odds any mainline game has ever offered. The rarer massive mass outbreaks trade rate for volume at about 1 in 315 base, covering a whole map at once.

Pokemon Let's Go shiny odds

Let's Go Pikachu and Eevee run on the catch combo system. The base rate is 1 in 4,096, and chaining catches of the same species is everything: at a 31+ combo the odds hit 1 in 341, with the Shiny Charm they reach 1 in 273, and adding a Lure on top squeezes out 1 in 256. The combo only counts consecutive catches of one species — run from everything else and never let the chain break. Because shinies appear in the overworld before you touch them, a combo hunt is mostly about patience and route planning.

Scarlet & Violet shiny odds

Scarlet and Violet keep the 1 in 4,096 base and offer three stackable boosts. The Shiny Charm (complete the Paldea dex) triples the rate to 1 in 1,365, a Sparkling Power level 3 sandwich gives 1 in 1,024 for thirty minutes, and clearing 60 or more Pokemon in a mass outbreak lifts that outbreak to 1 in 1,365. All three together reach 1 in 512 — the ceiling for the modern mainline games outside Legends Arceus. Note that sandwiches do not affect eggs; breeding hunts use the Masuda Method instead.

Sword & Shield shiny odds

Sword and Shield use the standard 1 in 4,096 base with the Shiny Charm at 1 in 1,365. The games also track how many times you have battled each species — hitting 500 battles with one species quietly adds extra rolls, and combined with the Charm the community-measured rate lands around 1 in 455. Gen 8 also introduced square shinies, a rarer sparkle variant: in the wild, a shiny has a 1-in-16 chance of the square animation, which makes a full-odds square shiny effectively a 1-in-65,536 event.

Brilliant Diamond & Shining Pearl shiny odds

BDSP stay faithful to the originals with a 1 in 4,096 base and the Shiny Charm at 1 in 1,365. Their signature method is the Poke Radar: chaining encounters in the tall grass builds shiny patches, and at the maximum chain of 40 the odds reach about 1 in 99 per patch — technically the strongest repeatable rate in any Switch-era game, though chains break easily and take real practice to maintain.

Pokemon GO shiny odds

Pokemon GO plays by its own rules. Most wild species sit at roughly 1 in 500, permanently boosted species like Onix and Sneasel sit closer to 1 in 64, and legendary raid shinies are around 1 in 20. Community Days slam a featured species to roughly 1 in 25 for a few hours. There is no charm or chaining — rates are set per species and per event, which is why GO hunters live and die by the event calendar.

FAQ

Pokemon Shiny Odds FAQ

What are the odds of finding a shiny Pokemon?

It depends on the game. From Gold and Silver through Black 2 and White 2 (Gen 2-5) the base rate was 1 in 8,192 — about 0.0122%. From X and Y onward (Gen 6+) the base rate doubled to 1 in 4,096, about 0.0244%. Boosts like the Shiny Charm, Masuda Method, outbreaks and catch combos can push the modern rate as far as 1 in 512, and Legends Arceus tops out at 1 in 128 with everything stacked.

What is full odds shiny hunting?

Full odds means hunting with no boosts at all — every encounter rolls the plain base rate. That is 1 in 4,096 in modern games and 1 in 8,192 in Gen 2-5. Full-odds hunts are the purist format: no Shiny Charm, no sandwiches, no combos, just raw probability. A full-odds shiny is considered the most prestigious kind because nothing tilted the rate in the hunter's favor.

How much does the Shiny Charm increase odds?

The Shiny Charm roughly triples your odds. In Gen 6 and later it takes the base rate from 1 in 4,096 to about 1 in 1,365, and in Black 2 and White 2, where it debuted, it improves 1 in 8,192 to about 1 in 2,731. You earn it by completing the regional or national Pokedex, and it stacks with almost every other boost — Masuda breeding with the Charm reaches about 1 in 512.

What is the Masuda Method and what are its odds?

The Masuda Method is breeding two Pokemon from games of different real-world languages — say an English Ditto with a Japanese Garchomp. Eggs produced that way get extra shiny rolls: about 1 in 683 in Gen 6 and later (from 1 in 4,096), or about 1 in 1,639 in Gen 4 and 5. Add the Shiny Charm and the modern rate improves to roughly 1 in 512, the best widely available odds in the mainline games.

Are shiny odds independent each encounter?

Yes. Every encounter is its own independent roll — the game does not track how long you have been hunting, and there is no pity counter or hidden guarantee. At 1 in 4,096, your ten-thousandth encounter has exactly the same chance as your first. That is why long dry streaks are normal: going 4,096 encounters without a shiny at full odds happens to about 36.8% of hunters.

What are the odds of NOT finding a shiny in 1,000 encounters?

Multiply the failure chance across all 1,000 rolls. At full modern odds (1 in 4,096), the chance of zero shinies in 1,000 encounters is about 78.3% — so only around 21.7% of hunts succeed that early. At the best stacked rate of 1 in 512, the chance of a dry 1,000-encounter stretch drops to about 14.2%. The calculator above computes this for any odds and any encounter count.
