Base Shiny Odds by Generation

Shiny Pokemon debuted in Generation II, and the base shiny rate only changed once in series history. Gold, Silver, and every game through Black 2 and White 2 rolled 1 in 8,192; X and Y doubled the rate to 1 in 4,096, and every mainline game since starts from that same base before boosts. Because each encounter is an independent roll, the full-odds shiny chance never moves.

Boost guide

How to Get the Shiny Charm

The Shiny Charm is a key item that adds extra shiny rolls to almost every hunt. It debuted in Black 2 and White 2, lifting the classic 1 in 8,192 rate to roughly 1 in 2,731, and in most modern games it adds two rolls — about 1 in 1,365 from the Gen 6+ base. Legends: Arceus and Legends: Z-A grant three instead. It stacks with Masuda breeding, sandwiches, outbreaks and chains, and you earn it by finishing your Pokedex; the milestone varies from a complete regional dex to research progress in the Legends games. Two exceptions: it does not affect Poke Radar shiny patches in BDSP, and no charm exists before Black 2 and White 2.

Shiny-Locked Pokemon

A shiny-locked Pokemon can never appear shiny, no matter how many encounters or resets you spend. The lock covers gift Pokemon, in-game trades, cover legendaries and story encounters that begin with a cutscene. It varies by game: Scarlet and Violet lock every headline legendary, Sword and Shield let you hunt most legendaries through Dynamax Adventures, and in Legends: Z-A only wild spawns and fossils can shine. Rule of thumb: if a Pokemon is handed to you rather than found in the wild, assume it is locked.

Pokemon Legends: Z-A shiny odds

Legends: Z-A keeps the modern 1 in 4,096 base, and its Shiny Charm adds three rolls instead of the usual two — wild encounters drop to about 1 in 1,024. Sparkling Power donuts stack on top at +1, +2 or +3 rolls by level, and the full stack (Charm plus a Lv. 3 donut) lands near 1 in 585, the best repeatable rate in the game. Only wild spawns and fossils can shine: fossil restorations stay at the plain 1 in 4,096, while static encounters, gifts, trades and scripted battles cannot be shiny. These fractions come from the community datamine of the Mega Dimension update, not an official table.

Pokemon Legends: Arceus shiny odds

Legends Arceus starts at the modern 1 in 4,096 base, then layers research bonuses on top: raising a species' Pokedex research to level 10 moves it to 1 in 2,048, and a perfect research page improves that to 1 in 1,024. The Shiny Charm adds three rolls on its own, and everything stacks. Mass outbreaks push it further: an outbreak alone jumps to roughly 1 in 158, and a perfect-dex outbreak with the Charm active reaches 32 rolls per spawn — 1 in 128, the best odds any mainline game has ever offered. The rarer massive mass outbreaks trade rate for volume at about 1 in 315 base, covering a whole map at once.

Pokemon Let's Go shiny odds

Let's Go Pikachu and Eevee run on the catch combo system. At a 31+ combo the odds hit 1 in 341, the Shiny Charm pulls that to 1 in 293, and adding a Lure on top squeezes out 1 in 273 — the best rate in the game. The combo only counts consecutive catches of one species, so never let the chain break. Shinies appear in the overworld, so a combo hunt is mostly patience and route planning.

Scarlet & Violet shiny odds

Scarlet and Violet keep the 1 in 4,096 base and offer three stackable boosts. The Shiny Charm (complete the Paldea dex) triples the rate to 1 in 1,365, a Sparkling Power level 3 sandwich gives 1 in 1,024 for thirty minutes, and clearing 60 or more Pokemon in a mass outbreak lifts that outbreak to 1 in 1,365. All three together reach 1 in 512, the modern ceiling outside Legends Arceus. Note that sandwiches do not affect eggs; breeding hunts use the Masuda Method instead.

Sword & Shield shiny odds

Sword and Shield use the standard 1 in 4,096 base with the Shiny Charm at 1 in 1,365. The games also track battles per species: 500 battles adds extra rolls, and with the Charm the community-measured rate lands around 1 in 455. Gen 8 also introduced square shinies, a rarer sparkle variant: in the wild, a shiny has a 1-in-16 chance of the square animation, which makes a full-odds square shiny effectively a 1-in-65,536 event.

Brilliant Diamond & Shining Pearl shiny odds

BDSP stay faithful to the originals with a 1 in 4,096 base and the Shiny Charm at 1 in 1,365. Their signature method is the Poke Radar: chaining encounters in the tall grass builds shiny patches, and at the maximum chain of 40 the odds reach about 1 in 99 per patch — the strongest repeatable rate in any Switch-era game, though chains break easily and take practice to maintain.

Pokemon GO shiny odds

Pokemon GO plays by its own rules. Most wild species sit at roughly 1 in 500, permanently boosted species like Onix and Sneasel sit closer to 1 in 64, and legendary raid shinies are around 1 in 20. Community Days push a featured species to roughly 1 in 25 for a few hours. There is no charm or chaining — rates are set per species and per event.

FAQ

Pokemon Shiny Odds FAQ

What are the odds of finding a shiny Pokemon?

The shiny chance depends on the game. From Gold and Silver through Black 2 and White 2 (Gen 2-5) the base rate was 1 in 8,192; from X and Y onward (Gen 6+) it doubled to 1 in 4,096. Boosts like the Shiny Charm, Masuda Method, outbreaks and catch combos push the modern rate as far as 1 in 512, and Legends Arceus tops out at 1 in 128 with everything stacked.

What is full odds shiny hunting?

Full odds means hunting with no boosts — every encounter rolls the plain base rate: 1 in 4,096 in modern games and 1 in 8,192 in Gen 2-5. No Shiny Charm, no sandwiches, no combos, just raw probability. Full-odds shinies are the most prestigious kind because nothing tilted the rate in the hunter's favor.

How much does the Shiny Charm increase odds?

The Shiny Charm roughly triples your odds: in Gen 6 and later it takes the 1 in 4,096 base to about 1 in 1,365, and in Black 2 and White 2 it improves 1 in 8,192 to about 1 in 2,731. It stacks with almost every other boost — Masuda breeding with the Charm reaches about 1 in 512.

What is the Masuda Method and what are its odds?

The Masuda Method is breeding two Pokemon from games of different languages — an English Ditto with a Japanese Garchomp, for example. Eggs get extra shiny rolls: about 1 in 683 in Gen 6 and later, or 1 in 1,639 in Gen 4 and 5. With the Shiny Charm the modern rate improves to roughly 1 in 512, the best widely available odds in the mainline games.

Are shiny odds independent each encounter?

Yes. Every encounter is its own independent roll — no pity counter, no hidden guarantee. At 1 in 4,096, your ten-thousandth encounter has exactly the same chance as your first. Long dry streaks are normal: going 4,096 encounters at full odds without a shiny happens to about 36.8% of hunters.

What are the odds of NOT finding a shiny in 1,000 encounters?

Multiply the failure chance across all 1,000 rolls. At full modern odds (1 in 4,096), the chance of zero shinies in 1,000 encounters is about 78.3% — so only around 21.7% of hunts succeed that early. At the best stacked rate of 1 in 512, the chance of a dry 1,000-encounter stretch drops to about 14.2%. The calculator covers any odds and any count.

Do shiny odds change between generations?

The base rate only changed once in series history. Gen 2 through Gen 5 (including the Gen 3 games) roll at 1 in 8,192; X and Y doubled it to 1 in 4,096, and every generation since keeps that same base. The Shiny Charm improves it to about 1 in 1,365 from Gen 6 onward.

How many encounters does it take to get a shiny Pokemon?

At full modern odds, half of all hunters find a shiny within 2,839 encounters, and 99% within 18,861 — the 50% and 99% points of the same 1 in 4,096 roll. Every encounter is independent, so your ten-thousandth try is exactly as likely as your first. The calculator above turns your odds into median and milestone numbers, plus a time estimate.

Which Pokemon are shiny locked?

Shiny locks fall on Pokemon that are handed to you rather than found in the wild: gifts, in-game trades, cover legendaries and cutscene encounters. The list changes every game — Scarlet and Violet lock every headline legendary, Sword and Shield let you hunt most legendaries in Dynamax Adventures, and in Legends: Z-A only wild spawns and fossils can shine. If a Pokemon is given to you instead of encountered, assume it is locked.
