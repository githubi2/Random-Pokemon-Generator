      How it works

      A Random Pokémon Generator Built for Real Runs

      Roll with real rules

      Every roll draws from all 1,025 species across Gen 1–9 with true random shuffling — no duplicates in a single team. Stack filters like a Nuzlocke ruleset: lock a generation, ban legendaries, limit evolution stages, or roll only shinies for a living-dex hunt.

      Filter down to the exact pool

      Ten-plus filter dimensions combine freely: 18 types, 9 generations, base-stat-total range, Pokédex color, regional forms, rarity, and evolution stage. The pool counter updates live, so you always know exactly how many Pokémon your next roll can produce.

      Share the exact squad

      Every filter — and your rolled team — lives in the URL. Copy the config link from this random Pokémon generator and friends, draft-league rivals, or stream chat see your exact squad, shinies included, then roll their own from the same rules. Every roll is saved to your history with its own share link.

      Instant tactical report

      Every squad gets an instant tactical breakdown: an S–D team grade, an 18-type defensive matrix, STAB offensive coverage, average base stats, per-Pokémon role tags, and shared-weakness warnings with actionable tips. This random Pokémon generator doesn't just pick your team — it grades it.

      FAQ

      Frequently Asked Questions

      Can I use it for Nuzlocke challenges?

      Absolutely. Filter by generation, type, evolution stage, or rarity to roll legal encounters, replacement Pokémon after a death, or a full randomized starter team.

      Which Pokémon are included?

      Every species from Generation 1 through Generation 9 — 1,025 Pokémon plus regional and special forms — with official artwork, base stats, abilities, and evolution data sourced from PokeAPI.

      Can I share the exact Pokémon squad I rolled?

      Yes. The Copy Config Link button encodes both your filters and your current squad — shiny variants included — into one URL. Anyone opening it sees the identical Pokémon, and every entry in your Roll History has its own Share button too.

      Does this random Pokémon generator save my rolls?

      Yes. Every squad you generate is stored locally in your browser's Roll History (up to 20 rolls) — hover any Pokémon to preview its details, click a roll to reload that exact team, or share it with a unique link. Nothing is uploaded or tracked.

      Does the generator analyze my team?

      Yes. Every roll produces a Team Tactical Report: an S–D grade, an 18-type defensive coverage matrix, STAB offensive coverage, average base stats, per-Pokémon role tags (sweeper, wall, tank…), and warnings about shared weaknesses with actionable tips.
