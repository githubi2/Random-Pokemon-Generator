How it works

How to Make Your Own Pokemon Card with the Pokemon Card Generator

Make your own Pokemon card in six steps

Step one, pick a template. Classic is the yellow base-set look from 1999, Modern follows the deep Scarlet & Violet border style, and TCG Pocket is the rounded style of the mobile game. Step two, choose a rarity finish: Common, Rare, Holo or Full Art. Step three, pick the card type: Pokemon, Trainer or Energy. Step four, set the art. Upload a photo and the card crops it into the art window on its own. Roll a random Pokemon and the tool fills the window with official artwork, plus the name, HP and type.

Step five, fill the details. The name goes under the HP bar, the type select sets the card's color theme, and the weakness select drives the little strength row at the bottom. Type the attack names and damage numbers, then a flavor line and an illustrator credit. Step six, the preview redraws with every keystroke. When it looks right, hit Download PNG: the card saves at 630 x 880, the ratio print services expect. Nothing gets sent to a server, so iterate as long as you like.

Why use a Pokemon card maker instead of a template image?

A blank template image does not reflow when your attack text wraps or your name runs long, so you end up pushing text boxes around for an hour. This maker redraws the card with every keystroke, and the layout problem never shows up. File quality is the second issue: pulling a template back out of an editor is hit or miss, while the download here is 630 x 880 at the exact 63 x 88 mm ratio, straight off the canvas. Edits are cheap too. Change your mind about the type or flip the rarity and the card rebuilds, no paint-overs. And because the state lives in your browser, the card is still there when you come back tomorrow.

Pokemon card generator templates: Classic, Modern and TCG Pocket

Classic is the warm yellow frame and dark name plate of the 1999 cards. Modern is a deep border in your type's color, with a bigger art window and a quieter stat zone. TCG Pocket takes the app's soft rounded frame and its white space. All three are drawn fresh in the browser; no official card layout gets copied.

Rarity stacks on top. Common keeps the plain frame, Rare adds a silver edge, Holo adds a rainbow edge, and Full Art runs the artwork edge to edge. Three templates times four rarities is twelve distinct looks, all from two rows of buttons.

Who makes custom Pokemon cards, and where they go

Pokemon fans make cards of their dream teams, their OCs and their fan characters. Content creators turn a screenshot or a meme into a card in seconds, then drop it into a Discord thread or a TikTok. Teachers and parents build reward cards and class rosters on the Classic template; a kid's name on a Pokemon card outdoes a sticker chart every time. Game designers mock up card concepts before paying for final art. The finished card goes everywhere: a friend's face with an inside-joke attack survives on a desk for years, a hyper-specific parody outlives the joke in any group chat, and the copy button packages the card with a link to this page. Anniversary cards and classroom reward cards get the same treatment: a physical card always beats a screenshot.

FAQ

Pokemon Card Generator FAQ

Does the Pokemon Card Generator cost anything?

Nothing to pay. There is no sign-up, no account and no watermark, and the template switcher, photo upload, live preview and PNG download are all part of the tool. Your photo stays on your device unless you choose to save or share the card.

Can I upload my own picture on a Pokemon card?

Sure. Upload any image: a selfie, a pet photo, a drawing, a logo or a meme. The editor crops it to fit the card art area automatically. You can also roll a random Pokemon for official artwork, or leave the art window empty and fill it in later.

What card styles and templates are available?

Three templates: Classic with the yellow base-set look, Modern with the deep Scarlet & Violet-era border, and TCG Pocket with the clean rounded app style. Four rarity finishes sit on top: Common, Rare with a silver edge, Holo with a rainbow edge, and Full Art, where the artwork fills the whole card.

Can I make a Trainer Card with this tool?

No, and that is deliberate. Trainer cards use a different layout: name plate, badge row and partner Pokemon instead of HP and attacks. They live on their own page, the Pokemon Trainer Card Generator, and you can jump there right from the card type row. The Trainer page links back here, so a matching set stays a half-hour job.

Can I make an Energy Card?

Yes. Choose Energy in the card type row and the generator switches to the energy layout: a full-color card in your chosen type, with the energy symbol centered and the type label underneath. Quickest way to give a custom deck matching energy.

How do I download my custom Pokemon card?

Hit Download PNG. The card saves at 630 x 880, crisp enough for social media, Discord or a print shop. The Copy Card Text button grabs a card description together with a link to this page.

Can I print the card I make?

Yes. The download is a print-ready PNG at the standard 63 x 88 mm trading-card ratio, so print it at home on card stock or send it to a print shop. The tool is for personal, educational and fan use; selling printed copies is not covered.

Does the generator save my work?

Automatically, in your own browser. Template, rarity, card text and your uploaded photo all sit in local storage, so a refresh or a return visit restores the card exactly as you left it. Nothing is uploaded anywhere, and clearing your browser data removes it.

What image formats can I upload?

PNG, JPEG, WebP and GIF stills, from anywhere: camera roll, camera, Google Drive, Dropbox, Pinterest, or a photo of a print. Images are resized on your device to fit the card art window, so even a huge photo stays smooth.

Does the Pokemon Card Generator work on mobile?

Yes. The form stacks and the preview sits on top on small screens, with touch-sized buttons. Build a card on your phone and finish it on a desktop later; the state follows you through local storage.

What can I use my custom Pokemon card for?

Anything personal: a birthday gift with a friend's photo, a meme card for a group chat, an anniversary keepsake, a classroom reward card, a fan-made character or a mockup for a custom deck. Personal and educational use is what it is for; commercial use is not covered.
