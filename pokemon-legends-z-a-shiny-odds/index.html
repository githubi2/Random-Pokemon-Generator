Mechanics

How Shiny Hunting Works in Legends: Z-A

Two levers move the rate in Z-A: the Charm and the donut. Here is how each behaves, plus the corners of the game where shiny hunting gets strange.

Sparkling Power donuts, level by level

Donuts baked from berries carry Sparkling Power, and each level adds a shiny roll to spawns that match the donut's type: one roll at Lv. 1, two at Lv. 2, three at Lv. 3. Two limits matter before you spend a good donut. The bonus only applies inside Hyperspace Distortions, so a Fire donut does nothing for the Water type beside you. And a Lv. 3 donut of any flavour forces the first close spawn that matches its type to be shiny — the game even posts a "Catch a Shiny Pokemon" task when you arrive in a Hyperspace Wild Zone, which is the indicator that one is on the map.

The Shiny Charm, and why it gives three rolls

Most games treat the Shiny Charm as a +2: it moves the base rate from 1 in 4,096 to roughly 1 in 1,365. Z-A hands out three rolls instead, which is why a charm-only hunt here sits at 1 in 1,024 — a third better than the same setup in Pokemon Sword and Shield Shiny Odds, where the charm alone gets you 1 in 1,365. The charm comes from Mable's research: clear every request in her thread and it is yours, with no Pokedex completion attached. It applies to every wild spawn in the game, Alphas included.

Fossils, Alphas and the Hyperspace scans

Fossils are the exception to almost every rule here. A restored fossil can be shiny at the plain 1 in 4,096, and it can come back as an Alpha at about a 1% rate, but the Shiny Charm does not apply to restores — a fossil hunt is full odds whether you own the charm or not. Five legendaries also slip past the usual lock: Latios, Latias, Cobalion, Terrakion and Virizion can turn up shiny through the Hyperspace special scans. Every other legendary stays locked, as the next section lays out.

Rarity

Shiny-Locked Pokemon in Legends: Z-A

Z-A draws its locks around story beats and handouts. Your starter cannot be shiny, and neither can any scripted battle — the Rogue Mega fights are hard locked, DLC ones included, along with gift Pokemon and in-game trades. Every legendary is locked except the five special-scan species that appear in Hyperspace. What remains open is the wild: ordinary spawns, Alphas (the fixed ones count as wild, not static) and fossils are the three sources a shiny can come from.

FAQ

Pokemon Legends: Z-A Shiny Odds FAQ

What are the shiny odds in Pokemon Legends: Z-A?

A wild spawn starts at 1 in 4,096. The Shiny Charm and a Level 3 Sparkling Power donut each lift a bare spawn to 1 in 1,024 on their own, and running both together tops out at 1 in 585 — the best repeatable rate the game offers.

How do you get the Shiny Charm in Legends: Z-A?

Complete Mable's research. Once every request in her thread is finished the charm is handed over, and from that point it adds three rolls to every wild encounter — three, not the usual two, which is why a charm-only hunt lands at 1 in 1,024.

Do Sparkling Power donuts stack with the Shiny Charm?

They do. The donut adds one roll per level and the charm adds three, so a Level 3 donut beside the charm reaches seven rolls and 1 in 585. Keep the types in mind: the donut bonus only reaches spawns that share its type, and only inside Hyperspace Distortions.

Can fossils be shiny in Legends: Z-A?

Yes. A restored fossil shines at 1 in 4,096 and has a small chance to come back as an Alpha as well. The Shiny Charm does nothing for it, though — fossils ignore the charm completely, so each restore is a fresh full-odds roll.

Which Pokemon are shiny locked in Legends: Z-A?

Starters, gifts, trades and every scripted battle, plus each legendary except the five Hyperspace special scans: Latios, Latias, Cobalion, Terrakion and Virizion. Wild spawns, Alphas and fossils are the sources that can still produce a shiny.
