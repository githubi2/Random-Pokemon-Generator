Mechanics

How Each Boost Changes the Scarlet and Violet Shiny Odds

Three systems do the work in Paldea: sandwiches, outbreaks and breeding. Here is how each behaves, and why raids sit outside all of them.

Sparkling Power sandwiches

A sandwich made with Herba Mystica grants its Sparkling Power for thirty minutes, and each level adds a roll to wild spawns that share the sandwich's type — one, two or three. Sandwiches do not reach eggs, and they do not apply inside raids. The working recipe for a hunt: build the sandwich around your target's type, eat it at the outbreak you have already cleared, and keep an eye on the timer.

Outbreaks: 30 cleared, then 60

A mass outbreak spawns like ordinary wild life until you start clearing it. Thirty knockouts add a roll, sixty add a second, which takes a bare outbreak to 1 in 1,365 by itself and to 1 in 512 once the Charm and a Lv. 3 sandwich join in. Defeating or catching both count toward the total, and only the outbreak's own species moves the counter — knockouts elsewhere do not register.

The egg ladder, and the raid exception

Breeding keeps its own arithmetic. A plain egg rolls 1 in 4,096; add the Charm and it lands at 1 in 2,048 — not the 1 in 1,365 the same charm gives in the grass, because of a roll the game skips whenever a bonus is active. Masuda Method eggs from two language-tagged parents roll six times for 1 in 683, and Masuda plus the Charm reaches 1 in 512. Tera Raids ignore every part of this: each normal raid is fixed at 1 in 4,103, and event raids can switch shinies off per species.

Rarity

Shiny-Locked Pokemon in Scarlet and Violet

Scarlet and Violet lock nearly everything the story hands you, and the list is longer than in older games: the four Ruin legends (Wo-Chien, Chien-Pao, Ting-Lu and Chi-Yu), the Loyal Three, Koraidon and Miraidon, Ogerpon, Terapagos, the Titan rematches, Gimmighoul outside its event raid, the scripted first encounters, and every Snacksworth legendary from the Indigo Disk. What stays huntable is the ordinary wild — every spawn and outbreak on the map — plus raid shinies when a seed lands your way.

FAQ

Pokemon Scarlet and Violet Shiny Odds FAQ

What are the egg shiny odds in Scarlet and Violet?

Breeding has its own ladder. A plain egg rolls 1 in 4,096; the Charm alone lifts it to 1 in 2,048; the Masuda Method rolls six times for 1 in 683. Masuda with the Charm is the best egg rate in the game at 1 in 512, and no sandwich helps — Sparkling Power does nothing for eggs.

Do sandwiches affect eggs or raids?

Neither. Sandwiches only reach wild spawns and outbreaks, and raids ignore boosts of every kind — a Tera Raid is fixed at 1 in 4,103 no matter what you carry into it. Raids are for species that will not spawn anywhere else, not for better odds.

What is the best shiny hunting method in Scarlet and Violet?

The Charm, a Lv. 3 sandwich and a 60-cleared outbreak on the same species — that is the 1 in 512 rate, and the fastest realistic setup on this page. Eggs with Masuda and the Charm reach the same ceiling at a slower pace per attempt, which suits hunts you can leave running.

Are shiny odds better in Tera Raids?

No. Normal raids roll 1 in 4,103, slightly worse than the wild base rate, and the Shiny Charm has no effect inside them. The only reason to raid for a shiny is that some species, like Gimmighoul during its event, are raid-only — and event raids can disable shinies entirely, so check the listing first.

What are the shiny odds with the Shiny Charm in Scarlet and Violet?

On a wild spawn, 1 in 1,365: the charm adds two rolls to the base of 1 in 4,096. It then stacks with everything else — an outbreak, a sandwich, or a Masuda breeding pair, where the same charm is half of the 1 in 512 egg ceiling.

Which Pokemon are shiny locked in Scarlet and Violet?

Everything the story hands over: the Ruin legends, the Loyal Three, the box legendaries, Ogerpon, Terapagos and the Indigo Disk's Snacksworth set. Gimmighoul is locked except during its limited event raid. Ordinary spawns and outbreaks always can shine.
